Winter driving

What do R1, R2 and R3 chain controls mean on I-80 over Donner Pass?

R1 means chains on every vehicle except cars and light trucks under 6,000 lb with snow tires on two drive wheels. R2 means chains or traction devices on everything except 4WD and AWD with snow tires on all four. R3 requires chains or approved traction devices on every vehicle, with no exceptions.

Drivers exempt from installing chains under R1 or R2 must still carry the required traction devices. (Caltrans) Caltrans says highways are often closed before R3 is imposed. Check the live chain reports on our California chain law page or the Donner Pass page before you leave.

How fast can I drive under chain control?

Follow the posted chain-control speed limit. Caltrans says it is generally 25 or 30 mph. (Caltrans) Caltrans can also turn back any vehicle it judges unable to travel the chain control area safely. (Caltrans)

Why does I-80 close over Donner Pass?

Caltrans identifies whiteout conditions, vehicle spinouts and avalanche work as possible reasons for temporary closures. (Caltrans) Spinouts can block lanes. Crews may hold traffic while the roadway is cleared.

When traffic is heavy in a major storm, crews may meter it, letting a limited number of cars onto the highway at a time. (Caltrans) CHP enforces chain controls and can cite and fine drivers who ignore them. (Caltrans) Its guidance does not specify a fine amount.

Can I take a side road around chain control?

Do not rely on GPS to bypass chain controls. Caltrans warns that side roads off I-80 and US 50 may not be plowed. (Caltrans)

What should I do before I drive Donner?

  1. Check the current level. Caltrans QuickMap or 1-800-427-7623. Our California chain law page reads the QuickMap feed every five minutes.
  2. Carry chains that fit your tires. Exempt at R1 or R2 still means carrying them.
  3. Use a pullout to chain up. Installers along I-80 are independent businesses, not Caltrans. Get a receipt and their badge number. They are not allowed to sell or rent chains. (Caltrans)
  4. Fuel up first. Fuel up before entering the mountains. Winter closures and traffic holds can last for hours.

Questions, answered

What does R1 mean on I-80?

Chains are required on all vehicles except passenger vehicles and light trucks under 6,000 lb gross weight with snow tires on at least two drive wheels. Those vehicles must still carry chains.

What does R2 mean on I-80?

Chains or traction devices are required on all vehicles except four-wheel and all-wheel drive vehicles with snow-tread tires on all four wheels. Those vehicles must still carry traction devices.

What does R3 mean on I-80?

R3 requires chains or approved traction devices on every vehicle. There are no exemptions. Caltrans says highways are often closed before R3 is imposed.

Why does I-80 close over Donner Pass?

Caltrans lists whiteout conditions, spinouts and avalanche control work as reasons for temporary closures in the Sierra. Spinouts, crashes, whiteouts and avalanche work can cause closures. Crews may hold traffic while the roadway is cleared.

How fast can I drive with chains on I-80?

Caltrans says chain-control speed limits are 25 or 30 mph. Follow the posted limit.

Can I use GPS to get around chain control on I-80?

No. Caltrans tells drivers not to use GPS to bypass chain control or find an alternate route, because side roads may not be plowed.

What is the fine for ignoring chain control in California?

Caltrans says drivers who ignore chain controls can be cited by the California Highway Patrol and fined. Its guidance does not specify a fine amount.
