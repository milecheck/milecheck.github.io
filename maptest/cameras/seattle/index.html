Live traffic cameras across Seattle

Seattle's freeways are among the most congested in the country, and WSDOT watches them with a dense camera network across the metro and the floating bridges.

Watch I-5 through downtown, I-90 and SR-520 across Lake Washington, and I-405 on the Eastside.

Every highway camera on this map comes straight from WSDOT and is tagged with the route and mile marker where the agency provides it. Tap a camera dot to open its latest image; tap the image to see it full-size. For cameras beyond the metro, see Washington traffic cameras or every highway camera.

Seattle city cameras

SDOT cameras watch city streets and intersections. The highway cameras on this map are WSDOT's. City cameras are the orange dots. Highway cameras are green.

These cameras show recent road images and refresh every few minutes. They are traffic cameras, not red-light or speed cameras.

For closures and crashes on the highways around Seattle, see Washington road conditions.

Source: Seattle Department of Transportation (SDOT). City street camera images: Seattle Department of Transportation.

Seattle traffic camera questions, answered

Where can I watch live Seattle traffic cameras?

Right here — the map above shows live WSDOT cameras across the Seattle area, each tagged with its route and mile marker. For the whole state, see Washington traffic cameras.

Are Seattle traffic cameras free?

Yes. WSDOT publishes its traffic cameras publicly, and MileCheck puts the Seattle ones on one map, with no account needed. The MileCheck app also has a Cameras page that lists the cameras nearest you.

Which freeways have cameras in Seattle?

Watch I-5 through downtown, I-90 and SR-520 across Lake Washington, and I-405 on the Eastside.

How do I check Seattle traffic before I leave?

Open the live map above and tap any camera near your route to see the road right now. Conditions change fast in a busy metro, so check just before you go — and in the app, the Cameras page lists the nearest ones and your mile marker updates as you drive.

Check the nearest Seattle camera before you go

In the MileCheck app, the Cameras page lists the cameras nearest you, and your exact mile marker shows as you drive on Seattle's freeways, plus live state DOT feeds on your route. The mile marker works offline and runs on CarPlay and Android Auto.
