Road data

What road data can I buy from MileCheck?

Highway mile markers for every US state and British Columbia, live road alerts through one API, and records of how long past alerts lasted. We sell them to companies that build fleet, dispatch, navigation, freight, insurance and media products.

Mile markers come as a file you license for a flat monthly or yearly fee. Live alerts are priced by calls a day.

What can I buy?

Mile markers

Every highway mile marker in all 50 states and British Columbia, by route and mile, as a GeoJSON and CSV file. You show it in your product for a flat monthly or yearly fee, with no call limits. We send an update when a state changes its markers. If you only need lookups, the API takes a GPS point and returns the highway, mile and direction.

Live alerts and conditions

Closures, crashes, construction and road weather from state and provincial DOT feeds. All 50 states plus British Columbia, Alberta and Manitoba, in one format through one API. Also active wildfires and Seattle drawbridge status. Priced by calls a day.

Records of past alerts

How long closures, crashes and Seattle drawbridge lifts lasted, by state and date range. Sent as a monthly file or through the API. In the 30 days to Oct 15, 2026, Seattle's Ballard Bridge lifted 259 times. The median lift was about 4 minutes.

Who uses it?

Dispatch and emergency services

Turn a caller's GPS point into the highway, mile marker and direction crews use. Use the file for your own maps, or the lookup for single calls.

Fleet and routing software

Show live closures and crashes in your dispatch screens through one API, instead of one connection per state.

Navigation and traffic apps

Put mile markers on your map from the file. Add live alerts from the API.

Freight and logistics

Use the records to see how long closures and crashes on your lanes lasted.

Insurers and claims teams

Use the records to see which closures and crashes were posted near a place around a date.

Media, cities and researchers

Use the records to count drawbridge lifts and road alerts by date. From Sept 25 to Oct 16, 2026 we recorded 15,961 crashes in 36 states and provinces. The median crash stayed posted 48 minutes.

Our mission

Become the trusted source for road information across North America, from today’s closures to next season’s roadwork.

What we do

We put every state’s Department of Transportation data in one place, on your phone, in your car and in your browser.

Our values

  • No ads in the app. Our website is free to use, so it has a clearly marked sponsor slot to help pay for it, without pop-ups.
  • Road data, not your data. We collect road data from state DOTs, not data about you. You don’t need an account, your mile marker is calculated on your phone, your trip history stays there, and we never sell your data.
  • Only what’s needed. Weather sends your approximate location to the National Weather Service, route planning sends your location and destination to Google, and wildfire alerts send a location rounded to about 7 miles only if you turn them on. Sentry receives anonymous crash reports, we count feature use without location or identity, and Apple or Google handles subscriptions.
  • Built for real roads. Nearly every feature exists because someone on a real road asked for it.
