    How does this work?

    Your browser finds your location after you allow access. MileCheck compares it with recorded DOT mileposts. It shows a nearby post, not an exact position between posts. Nearby roads, missing records and location accuracy can affect the result.

    This lookup runs once. Tap the button again for a new location.
