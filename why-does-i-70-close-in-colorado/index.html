Winter driving

Why does I-70 close so often in Colorado?

I-70 in Colorado can close after crashes, when vehicles become stuck on snowy grades, or during construction and avalanche work. CDOT meters traffic at the Eisenhower Tunnel. It also enforces traction and chain requirements during winter conditions.

Floyd Hill construction can cause lane closures and traffic holds. CDOT expects the project to finish in 2029. (CDOT) Check live CDOT events on our Eisenhower Tunnel and Vail Pass pages before you leave.

Why does traffic stop before the Eisenhower Tunnel?

That is safety metering. When traffic is heavy or conditions are poor, the approach signal cycles between red and green every four to eight seconds. The goal is to keep the 1.7-mile tunnel from filling with stopped cars, because emergency crews could not reach a problem inside it. (CDOT)

CDOT reports an average metering wait of about 20 minutes and an average additional travel delay of about 3.5 minutes. On busy days the eastbound on-ramps at Copper Mountain, Frisco, Silverthorne and Loveland are metered too. (CDOT)

Why does Vail Pass close?

Snow and stranded vehicles can interrupt traffic on Vail Pass. Colorado imposes a higher penalty when a vehicle's failure to comply with traction restrictions causes a lane closure. Avalanche control work can require temporary mountain-road closures. (CDOT) See the camera and current events on our Vail Pass page.

What is the Traction Law on I-70?

  1. When. Sept. 1 to May 31 between Morrison and Dotsero, and any other time CDOT turns it on.
  2. Tires. At least 3/16 inch of tread and an M+S, M/S or mountain-snowflake rating. AWD and 4WD vehicles must also meet the tire requirements.
  3. Or carry chains. Chains or an approved traction device also meet it.
  4. Chain Law. Under the Passenger Vehicle Chain Law, passenger vehicles need chains or an approved traction device. Commercial vehicles have separate chain rules. CDOT calls this the final step before closing the highway.

Breaking either law is $100 plus a $33 surcharge. If your vehicle causes a lane to close, it is $500 plus a $157 surcharge. (CDOT) Our Colorado chain law page has the full rules.

Why is I-70 closed at Floyd Hill?

CDOT is rebuilding I-70 from Floyd Hill to the Veterans Memorial Tunnels, west of Denver. The work involves lane closures, overnight detours and occasional traffic holds. CDOT says major rock blasting is complete. Westbound moves to its new alignment by the end of 2027, eastbound by the end of 2028, and the project finishes in 2029. (CDOT)

How do I know if I-70 is closed right now?

  1. Check CDOT. COtrip.org or 511 has closures, metering and Traction Law status. (COtrip) MileCheck reads CDOT's feed on our Colorado road conditions page.
  2. Look at the tunnel and pass cameras. A camera shows snow and traffic. Read the event list too.
  3. Check seasonal roads before you plan a detour. Many Colorado mountain roads close for winter. See Colorado seasonal closures.

Questions, answered

Why does I-70 close so often in Colorado?

Crashes, snow-related traffic problems, avalanche work and construction can close sections of I-70. CDOT also meters traffic before the tunnel and uses the Passenger Vehicle Chain Law as a final step before a weather closure.

What is safety metering at the Eisenhower Tunnel?

The approach signal cycles between red and green every four to eight seconds so traffic does not back up inside the 1.7-mile tunnel. CDOT reports an average metering wait of 20 minutes and an average added travel delay of about 3.5 minutes.

When is the Traction Law in effect on I-70?

From Sept. 1 to May 31 between Morrison and Dotsero, and at other times when CDOT activates it. Tires need 3/16 inch of tread and an M+S, M/S or mountain-snowflake rating, or you carry chains or an approved traction device. AWD and 4WD vehicles must also meet the qualifying tire requirement or use an approved traction option.

What is the fine for breaking the Traction Law in Colorado?

$100 plus a $33 surcharge. If your vehicle causes a lane to close, $500 plus a $157 surcharge.

Why is I-70 closed at Floyd Hill?

The I-70 Floyd Hill Project is rebuilding the highway between Floyd Hill and the Veterans Memorial Tunnels. Construction can cause lane closures and occasional traffic holds. CDOT says the project finishes in 2029.

How do I know if I-70 is closed right now?

Check COtrip.org or call 511. MileCheck shows CDOT events on its Eisenhower Tunnel and Vail Pass pages and on the Colorado road conditions page.
