Move over law · Indiana

What is Indiana's move over law?

The law is IC 9-21-8-35. Here is what it asks of drivers in Indiana.

What you have to do

  • On a road with 4 or more lanes (2 or more each way), change to a lane not next to the vehicle if safe. Otherwise slow to at least 10 mph below the posted limit.

Who it protects

  • Police
  • Ambulances
  • Fire trucks
  • Tow trucks
  • Road crews and construction
  • Snowplows
  • Breakdowns with hazard lights
  • Utility crews
  • Garbage trucks

What the statute says:

  • Police, fire, EMS (authorized emergency vehicles)
  • Recovery (tow) vehicles
  • Utility service vehicles
  • Solid waste haulers
  • Road, street and highway maintenance vehicles
  • Survey and construction vehicles
  • Any disabled vehicle with hazard flashers

Penalty

The penalty is set in IC 9-21-8-35. We could not confirm a dollar amount from an official source.

Why it matters

DOT crews and tow operators work beside moving traffic. Police officers respond to incidents on the shoulder. Arrow-board trailers mark work areas and lane closures. These workers, their vehicles and roadside equipment can be struck by passing traffic. Moving over and slowing down helps reduce that risk.
