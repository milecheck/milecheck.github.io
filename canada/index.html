Kilometre markers, province by province

Border wait times → What km marker am I at? → Road conditions: BC, Alberta, Manitoba →

Canada counts in kilometres, not miles — and each province posts its markers a little differently. Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, Alberta, Nova Scotia, and Northwest Territories km posts are live in the MileCheck app, and province by province we’re writing the driver’s guide to how each one’s markers actually work — the corridors, the signage, and the quirks. Green-bordered provinces have their guide.

Live kilometre-post data for Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, Alberta, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Yukon, and the Northwest Territories — from official provincial and territorial sources. The Trans-Canada is covered coast to coast, Victoria to Halifax. Positions are approximate. This data is coming to the MileCheck app in an upcoming update.
