Seasonal closures · Montana

Which roads close for winter in Montana?

6 roads in Montana close for the winter on a schedule the agency publishes, one of them a park road run by the National Park Service. Each page below shows what the MDT 511 feed is posting for that route right now and quotes the closing and reopening dates from the agency's own page. The dates move with the snow. Storm closures on roads that stay open all winter are on the Montana road conditions page.

US-212

Is US 212 between Pilot Creek and Cooke City open?

These 9 miles close to cars in winter and are usually open mid-May to early November. The Chief Joseph Scenic Byway (WY-296) stays open all year from WY-120 to Pilot Creek.

 US-212

Is the Beartooth Highway open?

Usually closes mid-October and reopens the Friday of Memorial Day weekend.

 MT-38

Is Skalkaho Pass open?

Closed for winter. MDT gives no set dates.

 MT-49

Is the Looking Glass Highway (MT 49) open?

Closed for winter. MDT gives no set dates.

 S-471

Is Thompson Pass (Montana S-471) open?

Closed for winter. MDT gives no set dates.

 Going-to-the-Sun Road · park road

Is Going-to-the-Sun Road open?

Park road. Usually closes around the third Monday of October and reopens late June at the earliest.

How to read these pages

A seasonal closure is a gate the agency locks for the winter, not a storm closure. MDT 511 posts storm closures and chain rules in its feed, and those show on the Montana road conditions page. A seasonal gate is announced on the agency's page and sometimes in the feed too. Each road page reads the feed for its route and lists whatever is posted, and says plainly when nothing is.

Closing dates in these pages are the ones the agency gives, with the date MileCheck read them. None of them is a prediction. When an agency has not posted dates for the 2026-27 winter, the page says that.
