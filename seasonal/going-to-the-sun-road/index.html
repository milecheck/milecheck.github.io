Is Going-to-the-Sun Road open?

Park road. Usually closes around the third Monday of October and reopens late June at the earliest. Below: what the MDT 511 feed is posting for Going-to-the-Sun Road right now, and the dates the National Park Service gives, quoted from its own page. This is a park road inside Glacier National Park. The Park Service runs it, and the state feed may not carry its gate.

What the National Park Service says

“The alpine sections of Going-to-the-Sun Road typically close for the winter around the third Monday of October, but this can happen earlier due to wintery weather. The entire road does not usually open until late June at the earliest.”

Questions, answered

When does Going-to-the-Sun Road close for the winter?

Around the third Monday of October That is what the National Park Service publishes, read October 2, 2026. The gate goes in when the snow does, so the date moves from year to year. No 2026-27 dates were posted on the source page when it was read.

When does Going-to-the-Sun Road reopen?

Late June at the earliest Plowing and avalanche work set the day, and the agency announces it when the road is ready.

Where exactly does it close?

Alpine sections; open year-round near the West Entrance. The rest of Going-to-the-Sun Road is handled like any other road in Montana.

Where do I check whether Going-to-the-Sun Road is open today?

MDT 511 is the official source for the state highway, and the Park Service page for Glacier National Park carries the park road's status. The block at the top of this page reads the same MDT 511 feed every five minutes and lists what is posted for Going-to-the-Sun Road. If the feed cannot be reached, the page says so instead of showing an empty list.
