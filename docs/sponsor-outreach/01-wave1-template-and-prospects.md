## Facts to keep straight when customizing

- Real number: 7,610 clicks in the last 28 days (Search Console), up 77% in three weeks.
  Cite the *page-specific* number once you pull it, not the site-wide number — a
  towing company doesn't care about site-wide traffic, they care about their page.
- Contact address the site actually uses: **sales@milecheckapp.com** (not leah@).
- Pricing is not published — "I'll send you a price" is accurate; don't quote a number
  in the first email.
- One sponsor per topic. Never imply two spots exist on the same page.
- Don't claim a specific page's traffic without checking Search Console for that exact
  URL first — site-wide numbers don't transfer to a single page.
