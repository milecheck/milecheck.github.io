# Corridor boards: a real strategic finding, not just a venue list

Spent the search budget on this tonight and it surfaced something worth knowing before
you spend real time on venue outreach: **major interstate corridors are the wrong place
to look.**

## Why

Searched I-35 (Austin), I-40 (Knoxville), and I-81 (Roanoke) for truck stops. Every
result was Pilot, Love's, TA, or Petro — national chains. A free-screen ask at one of
those doesn't go to a local manager who can say yes on the spot; it goes into a
corporate marketing/facilities approval chain, which kills the entire point of the pitch
(zero friction, near-guaranteed yes). Looked specifically for independent truck-stop
diners near Knoxville too — nothing surfaced that wasn't either a chain or a roadside
diner unconnected to any truck stop.

## What actually fits, and why it already worked once

The 3 boards that exist (`/boards/issaquah.html`, `/boards/blaine.html`,
`/boards/ballard.html`) aren't on generic interstate corridors — they're at specific
**destination bottlenecks**: a mountain pass, a border crossing. That's a structurally
different, better-suited venue type: a small number of local, often independently-owned
businesses sit right at a chokepoint everyone driving through has to pass, and the owner
can just say yes.

**This session's pass pages are the same shape of opportunity** — Little Cottonwood
Canyon and Anthony Lakes both have exactly that "everyone funnels through one point"
geography that made Snoqualmie/Blaine work.

One real lead from tonight, in that pattern: **Sinclair Gas Station and Convenience
Store**, 1065 E 9400 S, Sandy, UT — near the Highway 209 corridor into Little Cottonwood
Canyon. Sinclair stations are usually independently owned/operated (unlike Pilot/Love's
corporate locations), which fits the "local owner can say yes" requirement. I could not
verify exactly how close it sits to the canyon mouth beyond what search snippets say —
worth a 10-second map check before reaching out, not a guaranteed perfect fit.

## Recommendation

Don't keep searching interstate truck stops for this — it's the wrong shape of target
and further search time on it has the same diminishing returns the towing-prospect hunt
hit earlier. Better next step: your own local knowledge of a real venue at a
chokepoint (a gas station, diner, or visitor center right where the traffic funnels) is
worth more than anything more I can find blind. If you want me to keep hunting, the
right places to look are pass/canyon/border pages, not interstate exits.

The venue-outreach email template (free-install ask, from earlier) still applies as-is
once a real chokepoint venue is identified — nothing about the template needs to change,
just the target list.
