# Corridor board venue outreach — the easier first ask

Two-step sequence, not one hard sell:

1. Get a venue to install the board (free ask — nearly guaranteed yes)
2. Once it's installed, pitch a local sponsor on THAT specific screen (much easier —
   "your logo is already showing to real customers at this location," not an abstract
   web stat)

Step 1 email is the one to send first. It costs the venue nothing and gives you a real,
physical, photographable install within days — which becomes your best sales asset for
step 2.

---

## Step 1: Venue email (free install ask)

**Subject: Free live road-conditions screen for [venue name]**

Hi [name],

I run MileCheck (milecheckapp.com), a highway conditions site — live DOT cameras,
closures, and chain requirements, the same data as our iOS/Android app.

I'd like to put a free screen at [venue name] showing live conditions for [corridor —
e.g. "I-75" / "Snoqualmie Pass"] — the exact stretch your customers are about to drive.
No cost, no hardware to buy: it's a web page that runs on any TV or monitor you already
have, and updates itself.

Three examples already running: [milecheckapp.com/boards/issaquah.html] (Snoqualmie
Pass), [milecheckapp.com/boards/blaine.html] (the Blaine border crossing).

If you're open to it, I'll set one up configured for your exact location and send you
the link — takes a browser tab, nothing to install.

[Your name]
MileCheck
info@milecheckapp.com

## Step 2: Sponsor pitch, AFTER a board is installed (send only once real)

**Subject: The sponsor spot on the [venue name] road-conditions screen**

Hi [name],

Following up on the MileCheck board now running at [venue name] — the live [corridor]
conditions screen your customers see when they walk in.

There's one sponsor slot on that screen, currently unsold. It's the one thing every
driver in the building is already looking at. If you're interested, reply and I'll send
you a price and what the slot looks like with your logo in it.

[Your name]
MileCheck
sales@milecheckapp.com

---

## Notes

- Don't send step 2 to a venue that hasn't actually installed a board yet — the whole
  pitch depends on it being real and visible, not hypothetical.
- Good first venues: truck stops and travel centers directly on a corridor you've
  already built (I-75, I-90/Snoqualmie already has a demo, I-40, Ohio/Portland camera
  areas). Real venue names still need the same verification pass as the sponsor
  prospects — confirm they exist, are independent (not a corporate chain with a
  marketing-approval bottleneck), and have a real contact.
- The photo in `/partners/corridor-boards.html` (a fuel stop on I-90) is an *example*
  mockup, not a real customer — don't reference it as a case study when pitching.
