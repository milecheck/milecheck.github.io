# Sponsor prospect research — full batch (saved 2026-09-27)

Preserved here because chat history isn't a durable archive — this session already
compacted once, which drops the verbatim text of anything pasted before that point.
This file is the actual save. Original research: Leah, via ChatGPT, heavy-towing/mobile-
commercial-repair focus (rationale: "person looking at live highway conditions" →
"person who may need roadside commercial service" is an easy connection for a business
owner to understand, unlike CPMs/impressions/banner-ad framing).

Strategy: found 30 candidate leads, don't contact all 30 at once — start with 8-12,
learn what subject line/message gets a response, apply that to the rest. Rusty's Towing
and Atlanta Diesel Works are the first tests specifically because they have verified
direct emails, not just a dispatch phone line.

The framing that sells this, per the research: not "an ad" — "be the one towing/roadside
company presented to people checking [corridor] road conditions" / "own the sponsor
position on MileCheck's [corridor] page." Exclusivity is the pitch, not CPMs.
