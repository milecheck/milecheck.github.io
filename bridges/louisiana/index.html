How it works: live status comes from the Louisiana DOTD movable-bridge system (the same feed behind 511la) — whether each bridge is operationally open or closed to road and marine traffic, updated as DOTD changes it (not minute-by-minute lift events). Opening schedules come from the US Coast Guard’s Drawbridge Operation Regulations (33 CFR Part 117). Also see the Seattle, Florida, New Jersey, and Washington drawbridge pages.
