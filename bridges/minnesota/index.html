Minnesota drawbridge map — Duluth’s icon

Duluth’s Aerial Lift Bridge is one of America’s most-watched drawbridges — the whole span hoists 135 feet for lake freighters entering the Ship Canal. Upriver on the St. Croix, the historic Stillwater Lift Bridge runs a seasonal schedule. Tap any bridge for its rule.
