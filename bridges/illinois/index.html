Illinois drawbridge map — Chicago’s bridge-lift ballet

Chicago’s downtown river bridges lift in choreographed sequence every spring and fall for the sailboat runs to and from Lake Michigan — the city’s famous bridge-lift season. Downstream, the Illinois Waterway’s spans at Joliet work year-round for barge tows. Tap any bridge for its rule.
