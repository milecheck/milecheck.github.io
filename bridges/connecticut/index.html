How it works: the bridge list is cross-checked against CTDOT’s structure inventory (bridges that stopped moving — Congress Street, East Washington Avenue — are excluded). Opening rules come from the US Coast Guard’s Drawbridge Operation Regulations (33 CFR Part 117); Connecticut’s clocks are unusually strict, so the schedule is nearly as good as live status.
