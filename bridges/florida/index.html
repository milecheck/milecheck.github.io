How it works: live status comes from FDOT's statewide bridge sensors (mostly Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach today — FDOT is expanding coverage). Opening schedules come from the US Coast Guard's Drawbridge Operation Regulations (33 CFR Part 117) — the federal rules every drawbridge tender follows. A red bridge is raised right now; green-ringed bridges have a live sensor and are down; purple dots are schedule-only. Also see the Seattle and New Jersey drawbridge pages.
