How it works: opening schedules come from the US Coast Guard’s Drawbridge Operation Regulations (33 CFR Part 117) — Michigan’s bridges run on unusually regular clocks, which makes the schedule almost as good as live status. A few one-time drawbridges no longer open (Zilwaukee has been a fixed high-rise since 1988). Also see Seattle, Washington, Oregon, Florida, Louisiana, New Jersey, and North Carolina.
