Maryland drawbridge map — home of America’s busiest

Knapps Narrows at Tilghman Island opens about 10,800 times a year — the busiest drawbridge in the country, on demand, around the clock. Kent Narrows runs a seasonal hour/half-hour schedule; Annapolis’ Spa Creek bridge holds shut through rush hours. Every Maryland movable bridge — tap for its rule.
