How it works: opening schedules come from the US Coast Guard’s Drawbridge Operation Regulations (33 CFR Part 117) — the federal rules every bridge tender follows. Many Shore bridges open on signal all summer (boats generally have priority) but run restricted schedules off-season. New Jersey doesn’t publish a public live bridge-status feed yet; when one appears, this page gets live up/down like our Florida and Seattle pages.
