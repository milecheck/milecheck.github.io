Green Bay names its Fox River drawbridges after Packers legends — the Ray Nitschke Bridge and the Bart Starr Memorial Bridge both span the river downtown, with the Mason Street (Tilleman Memorial) Bridge alongside. Add the Superior harbor spans and La Crosse’s Black River crossings and Wisconsin has one of the Midwest’s densest movable-bridge sets. Tap any bridge for its federal opening rule.

How it works: opening rules come from the US Coast Guard’s Drawbridge Operation Regulations (33 CFR Part 117). Wisconsin publishes no live drawbridge status, so schedules are the tool here.
