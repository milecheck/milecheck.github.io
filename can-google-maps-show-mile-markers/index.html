Highway basics

Can Google Maps show mile markers?

Short answer: not reliably. Google Maps is one of the best tools ever built for getting from A to B — but "what's my current mile marker" is a different question than "how do I get there," and it's not one Google Maps was built to answer.

What mile marker am I at?

Use your location to find a nearby recorded marker. Or choose a highway on the map.

Why Google Maps doesn't do this

Google Maps is an address-and-directions app. It's built around place names, addresses, and turn-by-turn routing — it tells you where to turn, not where you are on a specific numbered highway right now. Mile markers are a different kind of data entirely: a continuous, route-specific position, posted by the state DOT every mile, that has nothing to do with your destination and everything to do with exactly where you are along the road.

Building that requires route-aware position data sourced from each state's own mile-marker signage — not something a general-purpose navigation app has built in, and not something it needs to answer its core question of "how do I get there."

What you'll actually see in Google Maps

Occasionally you'll spot a mile marker sign in Street View imagery if the capture happened to pass one, and if you zoom in closely enough on satellite view you can sometimes make out the physical signs on the shoulder. Neither of those is a live, continuously-updating readout of your position as you drive — they're static snapshots, not real-time tracking.

Why it matters

A mile marker is exactly what a 911 dispatcher, a tow truck, or roadside assistance asks for — not your GPS coordinates, not the nearest exit, your mile marker. It works anywhere on the highway, not just at an interchange, which is why it's the standard for reporting your location on a road. See mile markers vs exit numbers for how the two relate.

Questions, answered

Can Google Maps show mile markers?

Not reliably. Google Maps is built around addresses, place names, and turn-by-turn directions — it doesn't show a continuous, always-visible mile marker as you drive down a highway the way a dedicated mile-marker app does.

How do I find mile markers on Google Maps?

You generally can't, in real time. Some historical Street View imagery happens to catch a mile marker sign, and satellite view can sometimes show the physical signs if you zoom in closely enough on a highway shoulder, but there's no live, continuously-updating mile marker readout in Google Maps.

Why doesn't Google Maps show my exact highway position?

Google Maps is a routing and navigation app — it's optimized for getting you from A to B, not for reporting your continuous position along a specific numbered route. Mile markers require route-aware, DOT-sourced data that general-purpose mapping apps don't build in.
