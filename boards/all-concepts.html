MileCheck · Corridor Boards

Ten board concepts for three kinds of venue

Every board runs on data MileCheck already pulls live. The venue supplies a TV and wifi. Each concept below is shown at the size it appears on the wall, with the ad inventory and the real setup effort spelled out underneath so the revenue math is something you can actually run.

Read the spec strip under each board. "Ad slots" is what you'd have to sell at that venue. "Data" tells you what's live today versus what still needs building — sell the first kind, don't promise the second.

Venue type 01

Gas station, Issaquah — Snoqualmie Pass corridor

Last stop before the pass. Everyone in the building is about to drive I-90 east and is asking the same question: what's it like up there. Highest-value board of the three because the answer changes hour to hour in winter.

Option A — Pass Statussimplest to sell

One answer, readable from the coffee counter. Status is the whole board; cameras prove it.

Venue type 03

Bars & restaurants — Ballard and Fremont

The only venue type where the board makes the venue money directly. A raised bridge means a patron who was about to leave now has a reason to stay, and the bartender is the one who benefits. Lead with that, not with the technology.

Option A — Bridge Boardeasiest bartender sell

Every bridge, one glance. Reads from across a room, which matters in a bar.
