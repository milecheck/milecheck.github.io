Before you leave

Crystal Mountain is a resort, not a highway pass — the road that gets you there is different from the seasonal mountain pass just past it.

The access road stays open all winter

SR 410 from Enumclaw to Crystal Mountain Boulevard is the resort's access road and stays open through the ski season — that's the point of the road. WSDOT's live conditions feed tracks this exact stretch under the name "Crystal to Greenwater SR 410" (2,600 ft), separate from the seasonal pass report below.

Source: WSDOT road-weather reports, live via MileCheck's conditions feed.

Chinook Pass closes for the season — just past the resort

SR 410 continuing east of Crystal Mountain Boulevard climbs to Chinook Pass (5,430 ft), and SR 123 branches to Cayuse Pass (4,675 ft). Both close for the season, typically mid-November through late May, between Crystal Mountain Boulevard and Morse Creek near the summit — closed for avalanche risk, limited snow storage, and no winter emergency services on that stretch. The closure doesn't affect the drive to the resort itself, only the through-route toward Yakima.

Source: WSDOT mountain pass closure and opening dates.

Cell coverage runs out at Enumclaw

Coverage is limited to none on SR 410 between Enumclaw and Crystal Mountain Boulevard. Check the map above and download anything you need before that stretch — not after.

No service past Enumclaw. Download MileCheck and put your route in now, before you lose signal — the milepost sign and any saved alerts still work with no connection.

Parking needs a reservation on weekends

For the 2025–26 season, Crystal Mountain requires a parking reservation on Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays. Reserving within a rolling 7-day window is free, subject to availability; a season-long reservation costs $10 per day. There's no fee for the reservation itself outside that window structure — parking is separate from your lift ticket either way.

Source: Crystal Mountain parking and shuttle information.

Bus options, if you'd rather skip the parking reservation

A weekend shuttle runs from the Enumclaw Expo Center and Fairgrounds — check the resort's page for the current schedule and fare. Midweek, the Monday Ski Bus and Thursday Fun Bus pick up from the Fred Meyer parking lot at 1100 N Meridian, Puyallup, for $65 a single trip or $425 for an 8-ride pass.

Source: Crystal Mountain parking and shuttle information, midweek bus groups.

The mountain's own snow report

For lift status, snowfall totals, and the resort's own conditions, Crystal Mountain posts its own report directly.

Crystal Mountain driving questions, answered

How far is Crystal Mountain from Seattle?

About 84 miles southeast of Seattle, roughly a 2-hour drive: I-5 south to WA-18 east, then WA-164 east past Enumclaw to SR 410 east.

Is there cell service on the drive to Crystal Mountain?

Coverage is limited to none on SR 410 between Enumclaw and Crystal Mountain Boulevard. Check the live map above and download anything you need before you leave cell range.

Does the road to Crystal Mountain close in winter?

No. The access road (Enumclaw to Crystal Mountain Boulevard) stays open all winter for the ski area. SR 410 continuing east over Chinook Pass, and SR 123 over Cayuse Pass, close for the season separately — typically mid-November to late May — starting just past the resort.Source: WSDOT mountain pass closure and opening dates.

What is the elevation at Crystal Mountain?

Base elevation is 4,400 feet, summit 7,012 feet, per Crystal Mountain Resort's own mountain stats.
