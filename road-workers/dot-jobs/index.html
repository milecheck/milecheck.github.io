State DOTs hire highway maintenance workers, equipment operators, engineers, inspectors and office staff. Many maintenance jobs list a commercial driver's license (CDL), because crews drive plows and dump trucks. Each job posting says what it needs.

What does the job look like through the year?

In winter, crews plow and treat the roads, often through the night. In spring and summer they patch pavement, fix guardrails and signs, and mow along the road. All year they clear crashes and debris so the road can reopen.

If you drive past a crew, slow down and give them room.
