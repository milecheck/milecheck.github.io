Truck drivers

How much do truck drivers make in each state?

Your mile marker on screen as you drive. Plus live alerts and cameras in the MileCheck app.App StoreGoogle Play

Heavy and tractor-trailer truck drivers in the U.S. earned a median of $58,640 a year in May 2025, and about 2.1 million people do the job. Pay runs from a median of $49,320 in Louisiana to $70,100 in Alaska.

They move the freight on every interstate. Thank you.

How much do truck drivers make in each state?

Who is counted: This counts heavy and tractor-trailer drivers, the jobs that need a commercial driver's license. Pay is base wages from employers. Owner-operators who run their own business are not included.
