How much do EMTs make in each state?

Emergency medical technicians in the U.S. earned a median of $44,470 a year in May 2025, and about 180,500 people do the job. Pay runs from a median of $34,170 in Oklahoma to $66,410 in Hawaii.

They respond to crashes and get the injured to the hospital. Thank you.

How much do EMTs make in each state?
