Why buy it instead of building it

The data is harder to build than the app.

Every state publishes road data its own way. MileCheck already did the 53 integrations across 19 formats, keeps them running and fixes them when a state changes its format. With a file licence, lookups never leave your systems. An API lookup sends only the coordinates in that request. MileCheck has no driver accounts and keeps no trip history.

What building it in-house takes
  • 53 integrations in 19 formats. Many sit behind their own registration, API key and rate limit.
  • No common standard. No two states publish road data the same way.
  • Constant upkeep. Route names and mile formats change without notice.
  • Feed monitoring. Without it, your app serves stale data and does not know.
Who already works in mile markers
  • Fleet dispatch. "I'm at MP 184 on I-80." Telematics only logs coordinates. Fleet page →

Our mission

Become the trusted source for road information across North America, from today’s closures to next season’s roadwork.

What we do

We put every state’s Department of Transportation data in one place, on your phone, in your car and in your browser.

Our values

  • No ads in the app. Our website is free to use, so it has a clearly marked sponsor slot to help pay for it, without pop-ups.
  • Road data, not your data. We collect road data from state DOTs, not data about you. You don’t need an account, your mile marker is calculated on your phone, your trip history stays there, and we never sell your data.
  • Only what’s needed. Weather sends your approximate location to the National Weather Service, route planning sends your location and destination to Google, and wildfire alerts send a location rounded to about 7 miles only if you turn them on. Sentry receives anonymous crash reports, we count feature use without location or identity, and Apple or Google handles subscriptions.
  • Built for real roads. Nearly every feature exists because someone on a real road asked for it.
