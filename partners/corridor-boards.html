Questions

Before you try the board

What TV do I need?

You provide the TV and Wi-Fi. We test the board in your TV or streaming device's browser before setup.

What does it cost?

The board is free for your store. You provide the TV and Wi-Fi.

Who sells the sponsor slot, and can I refuse a sponsor?

MileCheck finds and handles the sponsor. If you object to a sponsor, for example a direct competitor, tell us in writing and we take it off your board within 5 business days.

How do I get paid?

Your store gets a share of sponsor payments when the slot is sold. Payment is monthly, for the money collected the month before.

Can I put my own specials on it?

Yes, the board can show your store's specials. Email the lines to sales@milecheckapp.com.

What if the internet drops?

The board needs Wi-Fi to fetch new reports. If it says "Reports unavailable", check the connection and reopen the board.

Can I end it?

Yes. Either side can end it at any time with 14 days' written notice. Email is fine, to sales@milecheckapp.com.

Who do I contact if something looks wrong?

Email sales@milecheckapp.com with the store name and what looks wrong. The road reports come from the DOT.
