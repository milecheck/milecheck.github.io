How it works

Three things to know before you ask for one.

For venues

The board is free. We configure it for your corridor and your location — drivers walk in, see exactly what's ahead on their route, and your venue becomes the place that knows the road.

For sponsors

One sponsor slot per screen — your brand alongside the one piece of information every driver in the building actually came in wondering about.

How it runs

Any screen with a browser — no hardware to buy, nothing to install or maintain. The board is a live web page that keeps itself current.

See one running right now — Snoqualmie Pass, the Blaine border, or the Ballard drawbridges. Each one is live data, not a mockup.
