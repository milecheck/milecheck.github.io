Last updated October 28, 2026 · EIA prices for the week of October 26, 2026

The U.S. average for regular gas was $4.27 a gallon for the week of October 26, 2026, down 1¢ from the week before. Diesel averaged $6.35, up 8¢.

Prices are averages across stations, including taxes. The station on your exit can be higher or lower.

Why don't all states have their own number?

EIA surveys stations each Monday and publishes the U.S. average, seven regional averages, and nine states it samples separately. The rest of the states are covered inside their regional average. California is broken out because its prices run well above the rest of the West Coast.
