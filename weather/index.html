About this data: weather warnings come from the National Weather Service live alert feed and refresh every 5 minutes. Warnings shown for driving-relevant events only — winter storms, tropical systems, floods, severe storms, and high wind — this is a road map, not a forecast site. Hurricane forecast tracks and cones come from the National Hurricane Center; the cone shows where the storm's center could go, not the size of the storm. Radar is NOAA NEXRAD data served by the Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Road closures come live from state DOTs via MileCheck (zoom in to a state to load them). Zoom and pan the map; the warning list updates to what's in view.
