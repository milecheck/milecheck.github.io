The storm is one half. The road is the other.

MileCheck shows your exact highway mile marker plus live DOT road alerts — weather closures included — in real time. Works offline in all 50 states.

App Store Google Play
About this data: weather warnings come live from the National Weather Service alert feed and refresh every 5 minutes — only driving-relevant warnings are shown (winter storms, tropical systems, floods, severe storms, high wind); advisories and marine alerts are left off. Hurricane forecast tracks and cones come from the National Hurricane Center and appear automatically when a storm is active. Radar is the national NEXRAD composite from NOAA via the Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Road closures (⛔) come live from state departments of transportation via MileCheck. Warnings are shown for driving-relevant events only — this is a road map, not a forecast site. Zoom and pan the map; the warning list updates to what's in view.
