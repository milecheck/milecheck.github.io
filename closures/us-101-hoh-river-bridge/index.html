US 101 Hoh River Bridge closure, October 1–6, 2026

Scheduled. Not closed yet. WSDOT lists it. Last checked Sep 30, 2026.

Closed to all traffic from 5 a.m. Thursday, Oct 1 to noon Tuesday, Oct 6. Inspectors found cracks in a support beam, and WSDOT is repairing them.

RoadUS 101, Washington
Mile markers176.5 to 176.8
BetweenForks and Kalaloch / Ruby Beach
DirectionBoth
SourceWSDOT incident WA-705229

There is no nearby detour. With the bridge shut, US 101 can't cross the Hoh River. Getting between Forks and Kalaloch means going back east around the Olympic Peninsula and coming down through Aberdeen. Plan on several extra hours.

WSDOT's posted way around: US 101 along Hood Canal, then SR 108, or SR 8 to US 12 into Aberdeen.

If the loop is your plan that week, drive it as two out-and-back halves, or after the bridge reopens. WSDOT schedules reopening for noon Oct 6. Check WSDOT for confirmation before you take this route. Check the official pages for changes to the schedule:
