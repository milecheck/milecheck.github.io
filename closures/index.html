About this data: closures come directly from state departments of transportation — the same official feeds that power the MileCheck app, normalized from dozens of different state systems into one format. Full road closures only (lane and shoulder closures are filtered out here; the app carries those too). Wyoming and Rhode Island don't publish usable feeds yet. If a state shows no closures, that usually means the roads are simply open — a quiet map is good news.
