How are the filters decided?

Every report needs a start and an end time from the DOT, and a span of 60 days or less. Today means it is active now or starts before midnight. Night means it overlaps tonight and either starts between 6 PM and 6 AM and lasts 14 hours or less, or the DOT posts a PM-to-AM window such as 9 PM to 5 AM. Weekend means it overlaps Friday 12:00 AM to Sunday 11:59 PM. Scheduled means it starts later. All times are the state's own time zone. Times come straight from the feed. Crews finish early and storms cancel work, so check the DOT before you leave.

Why is my state empty?

Some state feeds publish when a closure starts but not when it ends, and a few publish neither. The page says so when that is the case. Those closures still show on the live road conditions page for each state.
