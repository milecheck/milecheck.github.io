How does state DOT hiring work?

Rules differ by state and by agency. Most of what is published below comes from Oregon. Check your own state's rules.

  • Applications are scored against the posting. Oregon DOT says it scores applications against the "What we'd like to see" list in each posting, so answer every item with specific experience. Oregon DOT applicant information.
  • Seasonal and permanent jobs may have separate postings. Apply to each position you want.
  • Veterans get preference in Oregon. Oregon gives qualifying veterans and other eligible servicemembers hiring preference. For scored evaluations it is generally 5 percentage points, or 10 for disabled veterans. ORS 408.237 generally requires interviews for veteran applicants who show the required qualifications and skills, with a limited exception. See Oregon's veterans' preference page, ORS 408.230 and ORS 408.237. Other states have their own laws.
  • State hiring follows written policy. Oregon's is DAS policy 40.010.02.
  • You can ask for feedback. Oregon DOT postings include a recruitment contact. If you are not selected, you can ask how to strengthen a future application. They may not be able to give detailed feedback.

We do not know how any agency scores interviews beyond what it publishes.

Sources are Oregon DOT's Workday careers site (read Oct 23, 2026) and MnDOT's current job openings page (read Oct 23, 2026). Closing dates and pay come from the postings. Check the posting before you apply.
