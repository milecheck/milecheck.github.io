CarPlay & Android Auto

MileCheck on the car screen

See your mile marker and alerts ahead on Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Start and end a trip log from the car screen.

Now

  • Mile marker to one decimal, with the route you're on
  • Weather and road condition where you are
  • Crosswind warning at 20 mph and up, from the forecast wind and your heading

Alerts ahead

  • state DOT feeds on your road, in a list you can scroll: closures, crashes, construction, weather
  • Sourced from each state DOT and DriveBC

Trip

  • Start and end a trip log from the car screen
  • Keeps updating with the phone locked in your pocket

Details

  • Same screens on Android Auto
  • Shows in Spanish when your phone is set to Spanish
  • The phone app has the map, alert filters, and trip history. The car screen keeps it to what you can read at a glance.
