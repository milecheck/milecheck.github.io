Who it's for

Made for everyone who’s ever needed to say “I’m at milepost 142.”

01

Truckers

Dispatch needs an exact location. The mile marker just blew past you at 65 mph.

MileCheck reads your nearest mile marker in real time, with decimal precision. One number to report.

02

DOT workers, EMTs & first responders

You’re rolling to a crash and need to confirm the milepost fast — or you’re laying salt, sand, or brine and tracking each pass on a paper clipboard you have to keep dry.

Live mile markers, a DOT incident overlay, one-tap trip logging with notes. No clipboard required.

03

Road trippers

You’re miles into nowhere. Maps says “Highway 200.” That’s not specific enough to share with anyone who might come find you.

Tap the share button. MileCheck sends your route, direction, and exact milepost — a real location, not a vague pin in a green field.

04

Commuters

Closures, crashes, construction. You’d rather hear about them before you hit them.

DOT alerts on your route in 34 states, refreshed every few minutes. Filter by what matters.

What's in the box

Six things, done well.

01

Mile markers, all 50 states

Every interstate, US highway, and most state routes. Bundled offline so you keep working in no-service areas.

02

Live DOT alerts in 34 states

Closures, crashes, construction, weather, chains. Direct from 34 state DOTs — see the list.

03

Trip logging with path mini-map

Tap to start a trip. The app records your route as you drive and renders a clean mini-map under each log entry.

04

Multi-state coverage that actually works

Cross from Idaho into Montana. The state, the route format, and the live data source all switch automatically.

05

Offline-ready by default

Mile marker data ships with the app. No download, no setup, no “oops you’re in a canyon” moments.

06

Light or dark, your call

System-aware theme picker with six accent colors. Light by default for midday sun; switch to dark for night driving.
