About this data: incidents and perimeters come from NIFC/WFIGS, the National Interagency Fire Center's official feed — the same source behind government fire maps. "% contained" means how much of the fire's edge has a control line firefighters expect to hold, not how much of the fire is out. Amber tags mark prescribed (Rx) burns — planned, controlled fires, not emergencies. Zoom and pan the map; the fire list updates to what's in view, largest first.
