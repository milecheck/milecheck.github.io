The I-70 corridor

Utah to Maryland · over the Rockies. Here's what to watch, and where the weather actually bites.

Over the Rockies

I-70 runs about 2,153 miles from central Utah to just outside Baltimore, and its signature stretch is Colorado — climbing out of Denver into the high country through Vail Pass and the Eisenhower Tunnel, the highest point on the entire Interstate Highway System at 11,158 feet.

Where the weather bites

The I-70 mountain corridor west of Denver is one of the most weather-sensitive stretches of interstate in the country — snow, avalanche control closures, and holiday ski traffic all combine through Georgetown, the tunnel, and Vail Pass. East of the Rockies, the high plains of eastern Colorado and Kansas bring wind and occasional ground blizzards; further east, Midwest ice storms hit Missouri, Illinois, Indiana, and Ohio in winter.

I-70 questions, answered

Is I-70 open right now?

The live map above shows active closures and incidents along I-70, straight from each state DOT; a red banner appears at the top of this page if a stretch is fully closed. The most common winter closures are at the Eisenhower Tunnel and Vail Pass in Colorado. See the US closures map for everything.

How long is I-70?

About 2,153 miles from central Utah to just west of Baltimore, Maryland.

What is the highest point on I-70?

The Eisenhower Tunnel in Colorado, at 11,158 feet — the highest point on the entire U.S. Interstate Highway System, not just I-70. It's marked with a ▲ on the map above.

Does I-70 close for snow in Colorado?

Yes — CDOT runs traction and chain law restrictions and, in bad storms, full closures over Vail Pass and through the Eisenhower Tunnel corridor, especially on winter weekends with heavy ski traffic. Track your mile marker through it with the MileCheck app.
