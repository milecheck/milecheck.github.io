How should you read these reports?

These I-75 reports come from GoKY. Headlines and event type codes are shown as supplied by the feed. Reports missing coordinates may be omitted.

Future start times are labeled scheduled. Source update times appear only when supplied. An alert does not establish that the whole highway is closed.

No reports returned does not establish that the road is clear. A failed feed is labeled unavailable. Camera capture times may be unavailable.
