Chain and traction law · Colorado

Do I need chains in Colorado?

Colorado has a Traction Law and a Passenger Vehicle Chain Law. CDOT can activate traction requirements when conditions warrant. On I-70 between Morrison and Dotsero, the traction requirements also apply from September 1 through May 31. The levels

Traction Law

Passenger vehicles need all-weather or winter tires (mountain-snowflake, M+S or M/S) with at least 3/16-inch tread, or chains or an approved alternative traction device. AWD or 4WD alone does not meet it.

Source: codot.gov/travel/winter-driving/TractionLaw

Passenger Vehicle Chain Law

Chains or an approved alternative on every passenger vehicle, 4WD and AWD included. CDOT uses it as the last step before closing the road.

Source: codot.gov/travel/winter-driving/TractionLaw

The fines

  • Not meeting the Traction Law or Chain Law: $100 fine plus a $33 surcharge. Source: CDOT Traction Law
  • If it causes a lane to close: $500 fine plus a $157 surcharge. Source: CDOT Traction Law

Official pages read October 15, 2026. Fines and rules change, and the official page is the record.
