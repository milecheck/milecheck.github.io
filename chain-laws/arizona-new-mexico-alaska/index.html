Chain and traction law · Arizona, New Mexico, Alaska

Do I need chains in Arizona, New Mexico or Alaska?

These states do not use the California R1, R2 and R3 labels on this page. Check current road restrictions before driving. Follow posted signs and instructions from state transportation agencies. State 511 sites publish road conditions and some restrictions. The chain control map shows any chain report these states post in their feeds.

Arizona

Check AZ511 for Arizona road conditions and active restrictions. Follow signs and official road restrictions.

New Mexico

The applicable chain requirements and penalties have not been independently verified. Check NMRoads and posted restrictions.

Alaska

Alaska has seasonal rules for studded tires. Check current road conditions and any posted equipment restrictions separately. Check current Alaska DPS notices for studded-tire deadlines and extensions.
