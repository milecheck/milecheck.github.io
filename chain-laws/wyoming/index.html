Chain and traction law · Wyoming

Do I need chains in Wyoming?

WYDOT uses two chain law levels. They apply to passenger and commercial vehicles, on the routes WYDOT names. Current chain and traction reports from WYDOT appear below, followed by the rules and their official sources.

The levels

Level 1

Chains, adequate snow tires, or AWD.

Source: dot.state.wy.us/home/travel/winter/chain_law.html

Level 2

Chains, or AWD with adequate M+S or all-weather tires. Under Level 2, commercial vehicles must chain at least two drive wheels at opposite ends of the same axle.

Source: dot.state.wy.us/home/travel/winter/chain_law.html

The rules

  • WYDOT turns on chain restrictions on specific highway sections when conditions call for it. Check the current road report and the posted signs for active locations. Source: dot.state.wy.us/home/travel/winter/chain_law.html
  • WYDOT's road condition definitions explain each term used on wyoroad.info. Source: wyoroad.info/Highway/definitions.html

The fines

  • $250 for violating the travel restriction. $750 for a violation that results in a highway closure. Source: WYDOT chain law

Official pages read October 15, 2026. Fines and rules change, and the official page is the record.

$250 for violating the travel restriction. $750 for a violation that results in a highway closure.
