Chain and traction laws

Which states have chain laws?

Chain requirements, vehicle rules and published fines by state or province. Each page links to its official sources and shows chain reports from the state feed. Use the chain control map to see restrictions reported by state DOT feeds.

Washington · 4 levels

Do I need chains in Washington?

WSDOT posts traction and chain requirements by pass and direction.

 Oregon · 2 levels

Do I need chains in Oregon?

Oregon posts chain requirements by sign in snow zones.

 California · 3 levels

Do I need chains in California?

Caltrans posts chain controls in three levels, R1, R2 and R3.

 Colorado · 2 levels

Do I need chains in Colorado?

Colorado has a Traction Law and a Passenger Vehicle Chain Law.

 Idaho · posted by sign

Do I need chains in Idaho?

Idaho Code 49-948 requires certain commercial vehicles over 26,000 lb to use chains when the restriction is active.

 Montana · posted by sign

Do I need chains in Montana?

Montana may require chains or traction devices on signed highway sections.

 Wyoming · 2 levels

Do I need chains in Wyoming?

WYDOT uses two chain law levels.

 Utah · 2 levels

Do I need chains in Utah?

Utah can require traction devices on designated roads during severe winter conditions.

 Nevada · posted by sign

Do I need chains in Nevada?

Nevada's chain rules are in NRS 484D.515 and 484D.520.

 British Columbia · posted by sign

Do I need winter tires or chains in British Columbia?

British Columbia requires approved winter tires on designated highways from October 1 through March 31 or April 30, depending on the route.

 New York · conditional rule

Do I need chains in New York?

New York has no statewide chain law.

 Vermont · conditional rule

Do I need chains in Vermont?

Vermont can require tire chains on signed state highways in winter for vehicles over 26,000 lb.

 Maryland · conditional rule

Do I need chains in Maryland?

Maryland has no standing chain law.

 Pennsylvania · conditional rule

Do I need chains in Pennsylvania?

Pennsylvania does not require chains on passenger cars.

 South Dakota · conditional rule

Do I need chains in South Dakota?

South Dakota has no standing chain law.

 District of Columbia · conditional rule

Do I need chains in Washington, DC?

DC has no chain mandate.

 AZ · NM · AK

Do I need chains in Arizona, New Mexico or Alaska?

Arizona, New Mexico and Alaska do not use the California R1, R2 and R3 system.

What these pages do and do not say

They state the law and link the source. They do not say whether a road needs chains today. The sign on the road and the DOT posting decide that. Fine amounts are included where an applicable official statute or agency source supports them.
