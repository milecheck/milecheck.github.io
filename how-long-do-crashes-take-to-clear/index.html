How long does it take to clear a highway crash?

Your mile marker on screen as you drive. Plus live alerts and cameras in the MileCheck app.App StoreGoogle Play

A highway crash stays on a state DOT's feed for about 48 min before it is cleared, in the middle case. That is from 15,961 crashes MileCheck logged across 36 states and provinces from September 25, 2026 to October 16, 2026.

Clearing a crash means moving the cars, cleaning up debris and reopening the lanes. These times run from when the DOT first posted the crash to when it took it down.

How long do crashes take to clear in each state?

How to read this: each state's DOT decides when to post a crash and when to remove it, and some post unconfirmed reports. So compare a state with itself over time, not with other states. MileCheck reads every feed about every 16 minutes, so each time is accurate to about 16 minutes. Crashes seen only once are left out. States with fewer than 10 logged crashes are not shown. Left out because their feed timing does not show clearance: Kentucky keeps each crash posted for about a day. California crash posts are timed in fixed blocks of hours. Texas lists few crashes in its feed, and most matches were detours.

Where does this come from?

MileCheck reads the live crash and closure feeds of every state DOT and records when each incident appears and disappears. DOT feeds remove cleared crashes within hours, so this record does not exist anywhere else. We add a month to it every month.
