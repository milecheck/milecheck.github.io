Oregon camera questions, answered

Where can I watch live Oregon traffic cameras?

Right here — the map above shows live ODOT (TripCheck) highway cameras across Oregon, each tagged with its route and mile marker so you know which stretch you're seeing. For cameras beyond Oregon, see every highway camera in 27 states.

Are Oregon DOT traffic cameras free?

Yes. ODOT (TripCheck) publishes its traffic cameras publicly, and MileCheck puts them on one map for free — no account, no sign-up. To have the nearest camera follow you as you drive, the MileCheck app does it hands-free on CarPlay and Android Auto.

Which highways have cameras in Oregon?

Watch I-5 from Portland south over Siskiyou Summit, plus I-84 through the Columbia Gorge and the mountain passes on US-97 and US-26.

How often do Oregon traffic cameras update?

Most ODOT (TripCheck) cameras refresh every minute or two, straight from the DOT. Open the app image full-size by tapping it on the map above. Weather, snow, and traffic can all change between refreshes, so always drive to the conditions you actually see.
