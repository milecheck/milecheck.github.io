About this data: camera images come directly from state and provincial transportation agencies — Caltrans, WSDOT, ODOT/TripCheck, UDOT, MDT, ADOT/AZ511, NDOT/NVRoads, SDDOT, OHGO/ODOT, WisDOT/511WI, NYSDOT/511NY, DOTD/511LA, GDOT/511GA, SCDOT, AKDOT, FDOT, MDOT/MiDrive, Maryland CHART, WV511, DelDOT, ALDOT/ALGO Traffic, and New England 511 (ME/NH/VT) in the US, plus DriveBC, Ontario 511, 511 Alberta, and Transports Québec in Canada — the same official cameras the agencies use to monitor their own roads. Images refresh on the agency's own cycle (typically every few minutes). Camera positions are tagged with route and mile marker where the agency provides them; Washington State Ferries terminal and holding-lane cameras are included (blue dots). A few feeds (Delaware, Maryland, West Virginia, and Québec) are video-stream-only and don't appear as snapshot dots on this map yet. 24 US states + 4 Canadian provinces today; more coverage is being added. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence — British Columbia, Ontario, and Alberta; Québec camera data via Données Québec (CC-BY 4.0).

Federal networks: aviation weather cameras (grey dots, "Aviation" chip) come from the FAA Weather Camera Program (weathercams.faa.gov) — hundreds of camera sites, densest in Alaska and the mountain West, each with several fixed views; tap a site to open its live views on the FAA's own viewer. Park webcams (purple dots, "Parks" chip) come from the National Park Service (nps.gov) — tap one to see the park's image or open its live view on nps.gov. Imagery and data remain the property of the FAA and NPS respectively.
