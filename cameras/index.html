About this data: camera images come directly from state departments of transportation — Caltrans, WSDOT, ODOT/TripCheck, UDOT, MDT, ADOT/AZ511, NDOT/NVRoads, SDDOT, OHGO/ODOT, WisDOT/511WI, NYSDOT/511NY, DOTD/511LA, GDOT/511GA, SCDOT, and AKDOT — the same official cameras the DOTs use to monitor their own roads. Images refresh on the DOT's own cycle (typically every few minutes). Camera positions are tagged with route and mile marker where the DOT provides them; Washington State Ferries terminal and holding-lane cameras are included (blue dots). Fifteen states today; more are being added.
