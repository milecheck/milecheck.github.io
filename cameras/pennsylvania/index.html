Live cameras across Pennsylvania

PennDOT's 511PA cameras cover the Philadelphia and Pittsburgh metros, the Pennsylvania Turnpike, and the mountain interstates in between — more than 1,300 highway cameras statewide.

Pennsylvania camera questions, answered

Where can I watch live Pennsylvania traffic cameras?

Right here — the map above shows live PennDOT (511PA) highway cameras across Pennsylvania, each tagged with its route and mile marker so you know which stretch you're seeing. For cameras beyond Pennsylvania, see every highway camera in 46 states.

Are Pennsylvania DOT traffic cameras free?

Yes. PennDOT (511PA) publishes its traffic cameras publicly, and MileCheck puts them on one map, with no account needed. To have the nearest camera follow you as you drive, the MileCheck app does it hands-free on CarPlay and Android Auto.

Which highways have cameras in Pennsylvania?

Watch I-76 (the Turnpike), I-80 across the north, I-95 through Philadelphia, and I-79 and I-81 through the mountains.

How often do Pennsylvania traffic cameras update?

Most PennDOT (511PA) cameras refresh every minute or two, straight from the DOT. Open the app image full-size by tapping it on the map above. Weather, snow, and traffic can all change between refreshes, so always drive to the conditions you actually see.
