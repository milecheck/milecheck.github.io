Traffic cameras across Vancouver

The City of Vancouver lists its traffic cameras as open data. Its images stay on the City's own site, so each city camera here links to that view. DriveBC covers the highways and bridges.

Watch Highway 1 and the Second Narrows (Ironworkers) Bridge, Highway 99 through the Massey crossing and the Lions Gate Bridge, and Highway 91.

Every highway camera on this map comes straight from DriveBC and is tagged with the route and kilometre marker where the agency provides it. Tap a camera dot to open its latest image; tap the image to see it full-size. For cameras beyond the metro, see British Columbia traffic cameras or every highway camera.

Vancouver city cameras

City of Vancouver camera locations come from the City's open data. The images stay on the City's site, so tap a city camera and open its view there. The highway cameras with images on this map are DriveBC's. City cameras are the orange dots. Highway cameras are green.

These cameras show recent road images and refresh every few minutes. They are traffic cameras, not red-light or speed cameras.

For closures and crashes on the highways around Vancouver, see British Columbia road conditions.

Vancouver traffic camera questions, answered

Where can I see Vancouver traffic cameras?

On the map above. Green dots are DriveBC highway cameras, with the route and kilometre marker where the agency provides one. Orange dots are City of Vancouver cameras. For cameras across the province, see British Columbia traffic cameras.

Do I need an account to view the cameras?

No. You can view the cameras on this website without an account. The MileCheck app is a paid subscription with a free trial. It shows highway cameras and your kilometre marker as you drive. City street cameras are on this website only.

Which highways have cameras in Vancouver?

Watch Highway 1 and the Second Narrows (Ironworkers) Bridge, Highway 99 through the Massey crossing and the Lions Gate Bridge, and Highway 91.

How do I check Vancouver traffic before I leave?

Open the map above and tap a camera near your route to see its latest image. Images refresh every few minutes, so check the time on the picture. The app updates your kilometre marker as you drive.
