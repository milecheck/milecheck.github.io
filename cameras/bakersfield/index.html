Bakersfield traffic camera questions, answered

Where can I see Bakersfield traffic cameras?

On the map above. The dots are Caltrans highway cameras, with the route and postmile where the agency provides one. For cameras across the state, see California traffic cameras.

Do I need an account to view the cameras?

No. You can view the cameras on this website without an account. The MileCheck app is a paid subscription with a free trial. It shows highway cameras and your postmile as you drive.

Which highways have cameras in Bakersfield?

Watch SR-99, SR-58 and I-5.

How do I check Bakersfield traffic before I leave?

Open the map above and tap a camera near your route to see its latest image. Images refresh every few minutes, so check the time on the picture. The app updates your postmile as you drive.
