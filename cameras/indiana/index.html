Live cameras across Indiana

Indiana DOT's cameras follow the interstates that cross the state, I-65 and I-69 north to south, I-70 and I-74 east to west, and the I-465 loop around Indianapolis. The lake-effect corridor near Gary and South Bend has cameras on I-94 and the Toll Road.

Watch I-65 from the Ohio River to Gary, I-70 through Indianapolis, I-94 and the Toll Road along Lake Michigan, and the I-465 loop. In winter the Snowplows layer shows where INDOT's trucks are, each with the route and mile it reports, what material it is applying, and a photo from the truck's own camera where it sends one.

Every camera on this map comes straight from INDOT and is tagged with the route and mile marker where the DOT provides it, so you can tell exactly which stretch of road you're looking at. Tap a camera dot to open its latest image; tap the image to see it full-size.

Indiana camera questions, answered

Where can I watch live Indiana traffic cameras?

Right here — the map above shows live INDOT highway cameras across Indiana, each tagged with its route and mile marker so you know which stretch you're seeing. For cameras beyond Indiana, see every highway camera in 39 states.

Are Indiana DOT traffic cameras free?

Yes. INDOT publishes its traffic cameras publicly, and MileCheck puts them on one map, with no account needed. To have the nearest camera follow you as you drive, the MileCheck app does it hands-free on CarPlay and Android Auto.

Which highways have cameras in Indiana?

Watch I-65 from the Ohio River to Gary, I-70 through Indianapolis, I-94 and the Toll Road along Lake Michigan, and the I-465 loop. In winter the Snowplows layer shows where INDOT's trucks are, each with the route and mile it reports, what material it is applying, and a photo from the truck's own camera where it sends one.

How often do Indiana traffic cameras update?

Most INDOT cameras refresh every minute or two, straight from the DOT. Open the app image full-size by tapping it on the map above. Weather, snow, and traffic can all change between refreshes, so always drive to the conditions you actually see.
