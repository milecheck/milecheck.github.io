Live traffic cameras across Portland

Portland's ODOT TripCheck cameras cover the metro freeways and the Columbia River crossings into Washington.

Watch I-5 and I-405 through the core, I-84 up the Columbia Gorge, and I-205 on the east side.

Every camera on this map comes straight from ODOT and is tagged with the route and mile marker where the agency provides it. Tap a camera dot to open its latest image; tap the image to see it full-size. For cameras beyond the metro, see Oregon traffic cameras or every highway camera.

Portland traffic camera questions, answered

Where can I watch live Portland traffic cameras?

Right here — the map above shows live ODOT cameras across the Portland area, each tagged with its route and mile marker. For the whole state, see Oregon traffic cameras.

Are Portland traffic cameras free?

Yes. ODOT publishes its traffic cameras publicly, and MileCheck puts the Portland ones on one map, with no account needed. To have the nearest camera follow you as you drive, the MileCheck app does it hands-free on CarPlay and Android Auto.

Which freeways have cameras in Portland?

Watch I-5 and I-405 through the core, I-84 up the Columbia Gorge, and I-205 on the east side.

How do I check Portland traffic before I leave?

Open the live map above and tap any camera near your route to see the road right now. Conditions change fast in a busy metro, so check just before you go — and in the app, the nearest camera and your mile marker update automatically as you drive.
