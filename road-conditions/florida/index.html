Driving across Florida

I-95 runs the Atlantic coast, Miami, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm, Daytona, Jacksonville. I-75 comes up the Gulf side through Naples, Tampa's orbit and Gainesville, crossing the Everglades on Alligator Alley. I-4 links Tampa and Daytona through Orlando. Florida's Turnpike cuts the diagonal from Miami toward Orlando on its own mile numbering, and I-10 runs the Panhandle from Jacksonville to Pensacola.

What the season does to Florida's highways

When a storm approaches, Florida's evacuation orders, contraflow plans and road-status updates are all given by route and mile, and in the Keys there is exactly one road out. Outside hurricane season the hazards are afternoon downpours, fog on Alligator Alley, and drawbridges that stop traffic on the coastal routes. FL511 posts all of it by route and mile marker.

Florida facts, with sources

  • Crash clearance. From September 25, 2026 to October 16, 2026, 2,291 crashes on the FL511 feed were seen on at least two reads. A crash stayed posted a median of 49 min, and for the middle half it was between 32 min and 1 hr 35 min. Each DOT decides when to post and remove a crash, so compare Florida with Florida over time, not with other states. How the numbers are made.
  • Sleeping at a rest area. Only briefly. Cars can stay 3 hours. Commercial drivers can stay 10 hours. Source: Fla. Admin. Code 14-28.002. Florida rest area rules.

Before a Florida road trip

  1. Check the map above for anything red on your route, then open FL511 for the DOT's own detour and restriction text.
  2. Look at the road. The Florida cameras show the pavement itself, and most carry the mile marker.
  3. For the long haul, the I-95, I-10, I-4 and I-75 corridor pages carry alerts for every state the route crosses, not only Florida.
  4. Know how to say where you are. Dispatch, 911 and tow companies ask for the route, the direction and the mile marker. How to report your location on the highway.
  5. Carry the app. The mile marker works offline in all 50 states, and the same FL511 alerts show on your route as you drive.

What September looked like

In September 2026, MileCheck's archive of the FL511 feed held 225 unique reports, including 24 closure reports, 66 crash reports, 47 work-zone reports and 3 weather reports. The routes with the most reports were I-95, I-75 and I-10. Counts reflect what the feed publishes, so a state whose DOT posts only closures shows only closures. Read the September Highway Report.
