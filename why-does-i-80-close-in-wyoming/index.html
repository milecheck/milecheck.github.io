Winter driving

Why does I-80 close so often in Wyoming?

I-80 in Wyoming closes when wind and blowing snow reduce visibility, drifts cover the road, or crashes block traffic. Wind blows snow across the road. Visibility can fall near zero, and drifts can form. Crashes may block the remaining lanes.

WYDOT cites extremely limited visibility, drifting snow and crashes blocking all lanes as reasons for closures. (WYDOT, Dec. 4, 2025) Check the live status on our Wyoming road conditions page or the I-80 corridor page before you leave.

Where has drifting snow caused closures on I-80?

The stretch between Laramie and Walcott Junction, 77 miles across open country, is the one WYDOT built snow fences for. Within three months of that section opening in 1970, snow blown by winds up to 100 mph buried it in 27 places and closed it for 10 days. (FHWA) WYDOT's wind sensors on that stretch sit at Arlington and Elk Mountain.

Fences helped. WYDOT started building them along I-80 in 1971 and added about 40 more miles later. An FHWA snow-fence case study reported a 70 percent reduction in crashes during snowy, windy conditions on the studied section. (FHWA) The road still closes. Snow fences reduce drifting snow on the road. They do not eliminate crosswinds.

What does each WYDOT closure term mean?

What the sign or 511 saysWhat it means for you
ClosedNo travel. Turn around or wait in town.
Closed to light, high-profile vehiclesThis restriction applies to specified light, high-profile vehicles. Check the posted restriction for your vehicle and trailer. (WYDOT)
Weight-based wind closureClosed to vehicles under the gross weight on the sign. Used since 2023. (WYDOT)
Extreme blow over riskA warning, not a closure. Gusts of 60 mph or more where no closure signs exist, or lower gusts on very icy road. (WYDOT)

Rolling closureThe closure point moves back to the next town when a town fills up, so drivers aren't stranded. (WYDOT)

Does a wind closure apply to my car or RV?

Check the posted restriction for your vehicle. A car towing a trailer may also be affected. WYDOT says unloaded tankers, flatbeds without high-profile loads and school buses are typically less susceptible. Drivers must still follow posted restrictions. (WYDOT)

Weight on the sign is gross vehicle weight, meaning the vehicle plus everything and everyone in it. Heavier vehicles can go on, but WYDOT says they assume their own risk because the sensors may not sit where the wind is strongest. (WYDOT)

What happens if I ignore a closure?

For wind and weight-based closures, a first offense is a misdemeanor with a $1,000 fine and up to 30 days in jail. A second conviction within three years carries a $2,500 fine. For licensing purposes, that conviction is treated as reckless driving. This took effect July 1, 2024, and applies to any driver, not only commercial ones. (WYDOT)

Under W.S. 24-1-109(a), willfully ignoring a road closure can result in a fine of up to $750, up to 30 days in jail, or both. (WYDOT Fact Book 2018)

Why is the road closed when the gate is still up?

The gates are hand-cranked. At remote sites a crew may need up to 45 minutes to reach and lower one, often by leaving a plow route. Under state law the closure signs, including overhead message boards, are the notice. The Highway Patrol enforces it either way. WYDOT counts at least 23 gate crashes since 2020 and almost $180,000 in gate repairs over five years. (WYDOT)

How do I know if I-80 is closed right now?

  1. Check before you leave. WYDOT posts closures at wyoroad.info and on 511. MileCheck reads the same WYDOT feed on our Wyoming page.
  2. Look at the cameras. Check available I-80 cameras and the current WYDOT road report.
  3. Plan where you'd stop. Laramie, Rawlins and Rock Springs have services. Long closures roll back to the next town.

Questions, answered

Why does I-80 close so often in Wyoming?

I-80 in Wyoming closes when wind and blowing snow reduce visibility, drifts cover the road, or crashes block traffic. Wind blows snow across the road. Visibility can fall near zero, and drifts can form. WYDOT lists extremely limited visibility, drifting snow and crashes blocking all lanes as the reasons it closes roads.

What does closed to light, high-profile vehicles mean?

The road is open to most traffic but closed to vehicles that wind can push over, such as RVs, campers, moving vans, box trucks and empty or light trailers. Since 2023 WYDOT has posted many of these as weight-based closures, with a gross vehicle weight on the sign.

What is the fine for ignoring a wind closure in Wyoming?

Since July 1, 2024, a first offense is a misdemeanor with a $1,000 fine and up to 30 days in jail. A second conviction within three years carries a $2,500 fine. For licensing purposes, that conviction is treated as reckless driving.

What does no unnecessary travel mean in Wyoming?

The road is open but WYDOT recommends you do not drive it because of ice, blowing snow or both. Oversize loads are not allowed under this advisory.

What is a rolling closure?

When a closure lasts long enough that a town fills up, WYDOT moves the closure point back to the next town so drivers are not stranded where there are no rooms or services.

Can I drive around a closed gate if the gate is not down yet?

No. Gates are hand-cranked and can take up to 45 minutes to lower at remote sites. Under Wyoming law the closure signs, including overhead message boards, are the notice. The Highway Patrol enforces the closure whether or not the gate is down.
