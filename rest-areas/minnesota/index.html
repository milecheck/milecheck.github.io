Can you sleep at a rest area in Minnesota?

Your mile marker on screen as you drive. Plus live alerts and cameras in the MileCheck app.App StoreGoogle Play

Cars can stop for up to 4 hours where posted. Commercial drivers can stay up to 10 hours.

Cars

Up to 4 hours where posted, per MnDOT.

Trucks

Up to 10 hours in a row to meet federal hours-of-service rules, in stalls built for commercial vehicles.

Camping and tents

Not addressed in MnDOT's policy.

Signs at a rest area can set a different limit. Follow the posted sign. Turnpike and toll-road service plazas often have their own rules.

Source

Minn. Stat. 160.2721 and MnDOT rest area policy. Read October 15, 2026. This page summarizes the rule. It is not legal advice.
