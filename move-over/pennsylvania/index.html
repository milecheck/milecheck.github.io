Move over law · Pennsylvania

What is Pennsylvania's move over law?

The law is 75 Pa.C.S. 3327. Here is what it asks of drivers in Pennsylvania.

What you have to do

  • Pass in a lane not next to the scene if possible, otherwise pass at no more than 20 mph below the posted limit.

Who it protects

  • Police
  • Ambulances
  • Fire trucks
  • Breakdowns with hazard lights
  • Utility crews

What the statute says:

  • Emergency response areas (police, fire, EMS, traffic stops, utility crews in declared emergencies)
  • Disabled vehicles with at least two markings such as hazard lights, flares or signs

Penalty

Fine: Up to $500. Source: 75 Pa.C.S. 3327.

Why it matters

DOT crews and tow operators work beside moving traffic. Police officers respond to incidents on the shoulder. Arrow-board trailers mark work areas and lane closures. These workers, their vehicles and roadside equipment can be struck by passing traffic. Moving over and slowing down helps reduce that risk.
