What vehicles do CAL FIRE and Seattle Fire drive?

October 19, 2026 3 min read

CAL FIRE and the Seattle Fire Department both publish fleet records. The two datasets follow different rules, so the counts below describe what each one lists. Neither says how every vehicle is assigned.

How many vehicles does CAL FIRE have?

CAL FIRE is the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection. The state's 2025 fleet report lists 3,669 CAL FIRE vehicles. That counts self-propelled ground vehicles that had not been disposed of. Trailers, aircraft and boats are left out.

By type, the list has 1,695 medium-duty pickups and 940 heavy trucks. It also has 381 medium trucks, 294 SUVs and 150 pieces of construction equipment.

Fuel runs 2,100 gasoline and 1,281 diesel. Another 130 run on E85 and 13 are battery electric.

The data gives make and model, not the job. A count of International trucks is not a count of fire engines.

Source: California State Fleet, Dept. of General Services, data.ca.gov (2025 report year)

What does the Seattle Fire Department drive?

Seattle's city fleet list shows 234 Seattle Fire Department vehicles, trailers left out.

Fuel runs 124 diesel and 79 gasoline. Twenty are electric, and 10 of those are Nissan Leaf sedans. None of the electric vehicles are pumpers or ladder trucks.

Source: City of Seattle, Active Fleet Complement, data.seattle.gov (updated Aug. 18, 2026). Make codes in the file are abbreviated (PIER, EMER, AMLF, SPAR). We read them as Pierce, E-ONE, American LaFrance and Spartan.

Can you compare the two totals?

Not directly. California's count is a 2025 report of vehicles not yet disposed of. Seattle's is the active list as of August 2026. One is a statewide department and one is a city department.
