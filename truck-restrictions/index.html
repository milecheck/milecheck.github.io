Where are low bridges, weigh stations and truck parking on my route?

Pick a state. The map shows low bridges and weight-posted or closed bridges from the federal bridge inventory, and weigh stations, truck parking, truck stops, rest areas and runaway truck ramps from OpenStreetMap. Tap a marker for the details.

Approximate. Bridge clearances and postings come from the FHWA National Bridge Inventory (2025 file), measured at the last inspection. Weigh stations and truck stops come from OpenStreetMap volunteers. Signs on the road are the rule. Read the posted clearance and weight before you go under or over.

    How to read the map

    • Low clearance. "Under the bridge" is the clearance for the road that passes beneath it. "On the bridge" is the clearance over the deck, for example a truss over the roadway. Numbers are rounded down to the inch.
    • Weight-posted. The state posted a load limit. The inventory does not carry the tons on the sign, so the popup shows the inspection load rating and how far below legal loads the bridge rates. Use the sign.
    • Restricted and closed. Restricted means a limit other than load, like one lane or speed. Closed means closed to all traffic at the last report.
    • Weigh stations, parking and truck stops are mapped by OpenStreetMap volunteers. Hours and open or closed status are not live. Some are missing.
    • Railroad bridges over a road are not in the bridge inventory. The 11foot8 bridge on Gregson Street in Durham, NC is one. It does not appear here.

    Sources

    • Bridges: FHWA National Bridge Inventory, 2025 delimited file (2025HwyBridgesDelimitedAllStates.txt). Items 10, 53 and 54 (vertical clearance), 41 (open, posted or closed), 64 and 70 (load rating).
    • Weigh stations, truck parking, truck stops, rest areas and runaway truck ramps: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Tags used: amenity=weigh_station, amenity=parking with hgv=yes or designated, amenity=fuel with hgv=yes or designated, highway=rest_area, highway=escape. Pulled 2026-11-25.
    • Base map: Esri, USGS.
