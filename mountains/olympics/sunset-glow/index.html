Are the Olympics going to glow at sunset tonight?

Alpenglow happens when low-angle sunlight lights up a mountain summit after the valley below has gone dark. It needs a clear summit and clear sky toward the setting sun.

    Questions

    What causes the Olympics to glow pink at sunset?

    Low-angle sunlight travels through more atmosphere, scattering shorter wavelengths and leaving more red and orange light to reach the summit.

    Can the Olympics glow when it is cloudy?

    Yes, if the summit and the sky toward the setting sun remain clear enough for sunlight to reach the mountain.

    Does a Great glow forecast guarantee sunset color?

    No. Cloud conditions can change before sunset, and even a clear summit does not guarantee visible alpenglow.
