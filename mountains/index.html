How we know

Each page reads the cloud ceilings at the airports between you and the summit, the cloud layers at the summit from the MET Norway model, and whether that cloud sits below the summit. Low cloud and high cloud are scored separately. A thin high layer and a low deck can both read as 60% cloud, and only one of them hides the mountain.

The forecast covers three days. Cloud forecasts are not reliable past that.

The app

Mountain Visibility Forecast launched on iOS in September 2026. It has all four mountains, picks the nearest one from your location, Alerts are optional: tap "Tell me when it's out" and it sends a notification when the mountain becomes visible and one before sunrise or sunset light, two a day at most and none at night. Turn them off any time. It is useful for photography and for deciding whether to drive to a viewpoint.

Android is in testing. If you have an Android phone and want to be a tester, email feedback@milecheckapp.com.
