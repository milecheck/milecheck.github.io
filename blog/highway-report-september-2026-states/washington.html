MileCheck Highway Report: Washington, September 2026

by MileCheck · October 1, 2026

What Washington's department of transportation (DOT) feed showed in September 2026. Part of the national MileCheck Highway Report, September 2026.

✉ Get the MileCheck Highway Report by email, once a month →

How we got these numbers: These counts come from a daily copy of Washington's department of transportation (DOT) feed, Sept. 1 through Sept. 30, 2026. Each alert was counted once. An alert posted and removed between two daily copies is not counted. Not all states report the same data. Fire figures are as of Sept. 30, 4 PM Pacific. Border waits are from U.S. Customs and Border Protection, saved every hour. Readings over five hours were treated as feed errors.

Closures and roadwork

15 closures and 853 roadwork records were on Washington's feed in September, out of 993 alerts of all types.

The routes with the most alerts were I-405, I-5 and I-90.
