MileCheck is coming to CarPlay

June 6, 2026 3 min read

MileCheck is coming to CarPlay — and Android Auto — this summer. We're thrilled.

Apple approved our Driving Task entitlement on June 5, 2026. It's a real review process, not a checkbox, and getting through it means MileCheck belongs on the dash alongside dedicated navigation apps. Android Auto is planned for around the same time.

What's coming to the dash

The same data you already rely on — real-time mile markers from all 50 states, live road alerts from 42 state DOTs, closures, crashes, construction, chain requirements — on the screen in front of you while you drive. No picking up the phone. No second subscription. If you're already a Premium subscriber, it's included.

Truck drivers, DOT crews, tow operators, incident command — people who work in mileposts, not addresses — this is what we've been building toward.

Timeline

In active development now. We'll share screenshots and beta details as soon as they're ready. Target: this summer, both platforms.

Available now

The phone app is live on iOS and Android. All 50 US states for mile markers. Live DOT road alerts from 42 states. $9.99 per year with a 7-day free trial.
