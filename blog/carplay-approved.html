MileCheck is coming to CarPlay

June 6, 2026 3 min read

Apple CarPlay requires more than a developer account. To appear in the navigation category — the part of CarPlay that stays visible while you drive — Apple issues a separate entitlement called the Driving Task entitlement. They review the app's actual navigational utility before granting it. Most transportation apps never get it.

MileCheck got it on June 5, 2026.

What the Driving Task entitlement means

The short version: MileCheck will appear on the in-dash screen. Not just as a phone app the driver glances down at — as a native CarPlay experience that's visible while driving, with the same real-time mile markers and live road alerts the phone app delivers.

Getting this entitlement required Apple to separately review MileCheck's navigational utility. The category it unlocks is the same one used by dedicated navigation apps. It's not a checkbox. Apple makes a judgment call about whether an app belongs on the car screen, and they said yes.

What MileCheck on CarPlay will look like

The core use case is the one where looking at your phone is the wrong answer. A truck driver who needs their milepost for a weigh station check-in. A DOT crew logging which mile they're at. An EMT who needs to give their position without picking up a device. Eyes on the road, mile marker on the dash.

The in-dash experience is in development now. The same data layer that powers the phone app — mile markers from all 50 states, real-time alerts from 42 state DOTs — ships to the vehicle screen. No second app to manage, no extra subscription. If you're already a Premium subscriber, CarPlay is included.

Timeline

What's available right now
