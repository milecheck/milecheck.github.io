How we got these numbers
These are unique alerts published by Louisiana's department of transportation (DOT) feed, from a daily copy taken Oct. 1 through Oct. 31, 2026. Each alert is counted once. An alert posted and removed between two daily copies is not counted. Not all feeds report the same data, so compare each place with its own earlier months. Fire figures are as of Oct. 31, 4 PM Pacific.

Louisiana road fact

Did you know?

The Atchafalaya Basin Bridge stretches about 18 miles. Its two parallel bridges carry I-10 over the basin between Baton Rouge and Lafayette. They opened in 1973.
