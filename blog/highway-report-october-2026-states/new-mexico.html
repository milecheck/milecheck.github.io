How we got these numbers
These are unique alerts published by New Mexico's department of transportation (DOT) feed, from a daily copy taken Oct. 1 through Oct. 31, 2026. Each alert is counted once. An alert posted and removed between two daily copies is not counted. Not all feeds report the same data, so compare each place with its own earlier months. Border waits are from U.S. Customs and Border Protection, saved every hour. Readings over five hours were treated as feed errors.

New Mexico road fact

Did you know?

Route 66 once made a giant S-shaped detour through Santa Fe. New Mexico straightened it in 1937, cutting the state's Route 66 mileage from 506 to 399 and sending it straight through Albuquerque on Central Avenue.
