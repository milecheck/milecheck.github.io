What are the roads like for Thanksgiving 2026?

by MileCheck · November 2026

Alabama, Georgia, Tennessee and Virginia have announced they will pause most construction lane closures over Thanksgiving. AAA expects 72.9 million people to drive 50 miles or more from home between Tuesday, Nov. 24 and Monday, Nov. 30.

When is the worst time to drive for Thanksgiving?

The worst times are Tuesday 2 PM to 8 PM and Wednesday noon to 7 PM, according to INRIX. Thanksgiving Day itself is expected to be light.

AAA expects 81.5 million people to travel 50 miles or more between Nov. 24 and Nov. 30. Last year it was 81.6 million. INRIX, AAA's traffic partner, lists these windows for drivers.

How long is the border wait on Thanksgiving weekend?

Two flows set the weekend. Canadians drive south into the US for Black Friday, so the crossings into Washington, Michigan and New York fill on Friday and Saturday. The Canada Border Services Agency has warned that the US long weekend gets extra busy, with the longest waits back into Canada usually on the last day. From Mexico, people drive north into the US after the holiday, and Sunday afternoon is already the longest wait of a normal week at San Ysidro and Otay Mesa.

Are Washington State Ferries running on Thanksgiving?

Yes. Washington State Ferries runs on Thanksgiving Day, and some routes change their schedule. Last year Seattle/Bainbridge and Fauntleroy/Vashon/Southworth ran holiday timetables. Edmonds/Kingston and Point Defiance/Tahlequah had a few holiday changes. WSF marks the 2026 changes on each route's schedule and sends them by email alert.

Last year WSF expected about 300,000 riders from Wednesday to Sunday. Lines onto the islands were longest Wednesday and Thursday. Friday was busy both ways. Lines off the islands were longest Saturday and Sunday. WSF suggests an early morning or late night sailing, or a vehicle reservation where the route takes them.

Is BC Ferries changing sailings for US Thanksgiving?

BC Ferries' service notices list no Thanksgiving changes as of Nov. 21. Two notices fall that week. On Tue. Nov. 24, Village Bay to Swartz Bay runs a modified schedule. On Wed. Nov. 25, the 9:00 PM Swartz Bay to Fulford sailing leaves at 9:15 PM. Check the BC Ferries service notices or the BC Ferries app before you sail.
