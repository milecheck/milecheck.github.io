Ahead of Thanksgiving: What's Scheduled on the Highways

by MileCheck · November 2026

Alabama, Georgia, Tennessee and Virginia have announced they will pause most construction lane closures over Thanksgiving. AAA expects 72.9 million people to drive 50 miles or more from home between Tuesday, Nov. 24 and Monday, Nov. 30.

✉ Get the MileCheck Highway Report by email, once a month →

Where these numbers come from

Lane-closure pauses come from each state department of transportation (DOT) announcement, linked in the table. Travel numbers and drive times are from AAA and INRIX. This page does not count scheduled roadwork alerts, so check your state's 511 or the closures map before you leave.

VirginiaMost lane closures on interstates and major highwaysWed. Nov. 25, noon, to Mon. Nov. 30, noon. VDOT via Royal Examiner

Some work stays in place. Tennessee keeps long-term lane closures on some projects. Virginia keeps some semi-permanent work zones, and work on the I-64 Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel expansion can still affect traffic. Other states had not announced a pause as of Nov. 21.

When is traffic worst over Thanksgiving week?
