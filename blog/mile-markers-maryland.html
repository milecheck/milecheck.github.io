Highway Mile Markers in Maryland

July 4, 2026 4 min read

From the I-95 corridor to the Bay Bridge, Maryland's highways carry some of the densest traffic in the country. Here's how mile markers work in the state, the one quirk worth knowing, and how to see your exact marker plus live Maryland road alerts in real time.

How Maryland numbers its highways

Here's the quirk: MDOT's internal books log mileposts county by county, resetting at each county line. But the signs drivers actually see carry continuous miles along the route — so the number on the shoulder is the one that matters, and it's the one dispatchers and tow trucks use. Trust the sign. MileCheck's Maryland mile marker data matches the signed numbers, is bundled into the app, and works offline.

The corridors people actually drive

  • I-95 crosses about 110 miles of Maryland, from the Washington area up through the Baltimore tunnels to the Delaware line — the busiest stretch of the whole East Coast corridor.
  • I-495, the Capital Beltway, and I-695, the Baltimore Beltway, run on the local vocabulary of inner loop and outer loop rather than compass directions.
  • US-50 carries beach traffic east over the Chesapeake Bay Bridge toward Ocean City.
  • I-70 and I-68 head west through Frederick and into the mountains of Western Maryland.

Where mile markers matter most

On a beltway, "northbound" is meaningless — the road points every direction at some point. That's why Maryland traffic runs on inner loop / outer loop plus a mile marker or exit. And on the long approach to the Bay Bridge on a summer Friday, the backup's tail end is reported by milepost. Knowing yours is how you tell a dispatcher, a tow truck, or the friend picking you up exactly where you sit.

Weather and the Bay

Maryland's weather trouble is spread out: winter ice on I-68 and I-70 in the western mountains, summer thunderstorms that flood urban interchanges, and high winds on the Chesapeake Bay Bridge that can bring restrictions or full stops for high-profile vehicles. Every one of those situations is announced by route and milepost, and every one is a moment when knowing yours saves time.
