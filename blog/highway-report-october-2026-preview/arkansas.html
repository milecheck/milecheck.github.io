Where these numbers come from
These are unique alerts published by Arkansas's department of transportation (DOT) feed, from a daily copy taken Oct. 1 through Oct. 31, 2026. Each alert is counted once. An alert posted and removed between two daily copies is not counted. Not all feeds report the same data, so compare each place with its own earlier months.
