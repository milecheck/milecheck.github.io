MileCheck Road Report: September 1–25, 2026

by MileCheck · September 25, 2026

Partial month. This covers September 1 through 25. The full-month version, with final numbers, follows after September 30.

Data limitations:
  • A "0" for any category from any state means nothing showed up on that state's feed. It does not mean nothing happened.
  • States report at different levels of detail. A high number can mean a more detailed feed, not a busier month. Don't compare states against each other on these counts.
  • MileCheck archives every state DOT and DriveBC feed, two-hourly since September 8, daily before that. Counted once per incident id, no matter how many days it stayed on the feed.

30,835 road events across all 50 states and British Columbia, September 1 through 25, from MileCheck's archive of every state DOT and DriveBC alert feed. An event is one closure, crash, or work zone, counted once no matter how many days it stayed on the feed.
