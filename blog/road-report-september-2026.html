Data limitations:
  • A "0" for any category from any state means nothing showed up on that state's feed. It does not mean nothing happened.
  • States report at different levels of detail. A high number can mean a more detailed feed, not a busier month. Don't compare states against each other on these counts.
  • MileCheck archives every state DOT and DriveBC feed, two-hourly since September 8, daily before that. Counted once per incident id, no matter how many days it stayed on the feed.
