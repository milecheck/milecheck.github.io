Check it before winter, not during it

Most jump starters hold a charge for months sitting in the trunk, but "months" isn't "forever." Charge it fully before the first cold snap — cold weather is exactly when a car battery is most likely to need it, and a jump starter with a dead battery of its own is just extra weight in the trunk. Questions, answered

Is a jump starter better than jumper cables?

A jump starter lets you start your own car without needing another car and a second driver to help. Jumper cables still work and cost less, but they only help if someone else pulls up next to you.

What peak amp rating do I need?

Around 1,000 peak amps covers most gas engines up to about 6.0 liters. Larger trucks, V8s, and diesel engines need a higher-rated unit, typically 2,000 peak amps or more.

How often do I need to recharge a jump starter?

Most hold a charge for months in storage, but check it every season — a dead jump starter in the trunk does nothing. Charge it fully before winter and again partway through if you haven't used it.
