What it does

  • Shows the mile marker you're currently passing, updated live
  • Clips to an air vent — no wiring, no phone mount blocking your view
  • Same 50-state milepost data already in the MileCheck app

How it updates

The display itself has no GPS chip and no SIM card — that's what keeps it small and the build simple. Your phone does the work: MileCheck runs in the background, tracks your GPS position the same way it does on the Mile tab, and figures out your current mile marker. It then sends that number to the display over Bluetooth, the same way a phone talks to a Bluetooth speaker.

So the display is just a screen and a Bluetooth radio — the phone stays in your pocket or cupholder doing the GPS and lookup work it already does for the app, and the dash unit just shows what it's told. No pairing steps beyond the first setup, no data plan on the device, no separate subscription.
