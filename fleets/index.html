From coordinates to mile markers

A driver reports “I-80, mile 184.” You need to find that stretch.

Vehicle coordinates and highway mile markers describe location in different ways. MileCheck adds a nearby mile-marker reference to a highway position, giving dispatch another way to match a driver’s report with road information.

Bring state DOT feeds into that view to check reported conditions along the route. Alert types and location details depend on the state’s feed. Mile-marker positions are approximate and may differ from roadside signs.
