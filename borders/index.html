How to read it: waits are live for the direction you've picked — into the US (from US CBP, all crossings) or, on the Canadian border, into Canada (from Canada's CBSA, the busiest crossings). Mexico shows into-US only — Mexico's agency doesn't publish southbound waits. The number is the delay in minutes for the lane type you've picked (Passenger = personal vehicles, Commercial = trucks). Green is under 15 minutes, amber is 15–45, red is 45+. A dash means the lane isn't reporting or is closed. The Mexico/Canada toggle filters which border you're looking at. Tap a crossing to center the map. Data can lag real conditions by a few minutes — always follow posted signs and officers at the border.
