What vehicles do state DOTs and police drive?

Your mile marker on screen as you drive. Plus live alerts and cameras in the MileCheck app.App StoreGoogle Play

The California Highway Patrol's most common vehicle is the Dodge Charger. It had 1,535 of them in 2025, out of 4,320 vehicles. Caltrans, California's DOT, mostly drives pickups and heavy trucks. Its most common are the Chevrolet Silverado pickup and the International 7000 truck.

These counts come from public fleet records. California publishes its state agencies' vehicles once a year. Seattle publishes a list of its city fleet. There is no free official count of police or DOT vehicles for the whole country, so this page sticks to fleets that publish their lists.

How has the CHP patrol fleet changed since 2020?
