Corridor board sponsorships

A corridor board is a TV in a truck stop, travel center or diner that shows live road conditions for the highway outside. Closures, crashes, chain controls, cameras and weather, straight from the state DOT feeds MileCheck uses in the app.

Each board has one sponsor slot. Your name sits next to the thing every driver in the building came in to check.

Claim a board

MileCheck is an AWS Partner Network member and runs a live add-in on the Geotab Marketplace.

How corridor boards work

  1. Pick a highway or town. Boards are set up for one stretch of road, like I-84 at Pendleton or I-90 over Snoqualmie Pass.
  2. Send your logo and one line. Your business name, a sentence, and where you want people to go.
  3. Your slot runs on the board. One sponsor per screen. The board runs all day and updates itself.

Pick a board

Each board shows live data for one stretch of road and has one sponsor slot. Open one to see what drivers see. The venue and sponsor names on the demos are examples.
