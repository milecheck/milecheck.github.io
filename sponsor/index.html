Website sponsorships

milecheckapp.com is where drivers check a road before they drive it: live cameras, pass conditions, drawbridge status, interstate corridors, and how mile markers work in every state. A sponsor slot sits at the top of those pages. Website only. The app has no ads.

Sponsorships are sold by topic, not by state: the camera pages, the corridor pages, the pass pages, the drawbridge pages, the mile-marker guides, the highway-basics guides, the reports, the front page, the Mountain Visibility pages, or the Seattle Drawbridges pages. One sponsor per topic, so a tire brand and a towing company are never side by side on the same page.

The offering Pricing Traffic

What a sponsor gets

Your logo, one sentence, and a link, in the slot at the top of every page in the topic. Nothing follows the reader around, and no third-party script runs. See the slot on any page: Snoqualmie Pass, Ohio cameras, Washington drawbridges.

Pricing

Email and say which topic you want. You get the price, the page list, and the numbers for those pages.
