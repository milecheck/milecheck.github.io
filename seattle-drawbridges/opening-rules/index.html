A drawbridge opens on signal. A boat sounds its horn or calls the tender, and the bridge lifts. The rules below are the exceptions, and the exceptions are what matter to a driver: the hours a bridge does not have to open for small boats, how long a tender can hold a lift for traffic, and what happens at night.

Ballard Bridge

  • Opens on signal for boats.
  • On weekdays, except federal holidays other than Columbus Day, from 7 to 9 a.m. and 4 to 6 p.m. it need not open for a vessel under 1,000 tons, unless that vessel is towing one of 1,000 gross tons or more.
  • The tender may hold an opening up to 10 minutes to pass backed-up road traffic, but must open without delay for a vessel engaged in towing.
  • From 11 p.m. to 7 a.m. it opens if at least one hour's notice is given to the tender at the Fremont Bridge.

The draws of the Ballard Bridge, mile 1.1, Fremont Bridge, mile 2.6, and University Bridge, mile 4.3, shall open on signal, except that: (1) The draws need not be opened for a period of up to 10 minutes after receiving an opening request, if needed to pass accumulated vehicular traffic. However, the draws shall open without delay, when requested by vessels engaged in towing operations. (2) The draws need not open from 7 a.m. to 9 a.m. and from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m. Monday through Friday, except all Federal holidays but Columbus Day for any vessel of less than 1000 tons, unless the vessel has in tow a vessel of 1000 gross tons or over. (3) Between the hours of 11 p.m. and 7 a.m. the draws shall open if at least one hour notice is given by telephone, radiotelephone, or otherwise to the drawtender at the Fremont Avenue Bridge. 33 CFR 117.1051(d)

University Bridge

  • Opens on signal for boats.
  • On weekdays, except federal holidays other than Columbus Day, from 7 to 9 a.m. and 4 to 6 p.m. it need not open for a vessel under 1,000 tons, unless that vessel is towing one of 1,000 gross tons or more.

  • From 11 p.m. to 7 a.m. it opens if at least one hour's notice is given to the tender at the Fremont Bridge.
  • Seattle publishes no live up or down status for this bridge. The rule is all anyone has.

The draws of the Ballard Bridge, mile 1.1, Fremont Bridge, mile 2.6, and University Bridge, mile 4.3, shall open on signal, except that: (1) The draws need not be opened for a period of up to 10 minutes after receiving an opening request, if needed to pass accumulated vehicular traffic. Montlake Bridge

  • Opens on signal for boats.
  • The tender may hold an opening up to 10 minutes to pass backed-up road traffic, but must open without delay for a vessel engaged in towing.
  • For a vessel under 1,000 gross tons that is not towing one of 1,000 gross tons or more, on weekdays except federal holidays, it need not open from 7 to 9 a.m. and 3:30 to 6:30 p.m. between April 30 and September 1, or from 7 to 10 a.m. and 3:30 to 7 p.m. between September 1 and April 30.
  • For those same vessels on those same weekdays, from 12:30 to 3:30 p.m. and 6 to 6:30 p.m. it need open only on the hour and the half hour.

The draw of the Montlake Bridge, mile 5.2, shall open on signal, except that: (1) The draw need not open for a period of up to 10 minutes after receiving an opening request, if needed to pass accumulated vehicular traffic. However, the draw shall open without delay, when requested by vessels engaged in towing operations. (2) For any vessel or watercraft of less than 1,000 gross tons, unless the vessel has in tow a vessel of 1,000 gross tons or over, from Monday through Friday, except Federal Holidays: (i) The draw need not open from 7 a.m. to 9 a.m. and from 3:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. from April 30 to September 1 and from 7 a.m. to 10 a.m. and from 3:30 p.m. to 7 p.m. from September 1 to April 30. (ii) The draw need open only on the hour and half hour from 12:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m. and from 6 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. 33 CFR 117.1051(e)

First Avenue South Bridge

  • Opens on signal for boats.
  • On weekdays, except federal holidays other than Columbus Day, from 6 to 9 a.m. and 3 to 6 p.m. it need not open, but it opens at any time for a vessel of 5,000 gross tons or more, a vessel towing one, or a vessel on its way to pick one up for towing.

The draws of each bridge across the Duwamish Waterway shall open on signal, except as follows: (1) From Monday through Friday, except all Federal holidays but Columbus Day, the draws of the First Avenue South Bridges, mile 2.5, need not be opened for the passage of vessels from 6 a.m. to 9 a.m. and from 3 p.m. to 6 p.m., except: The draws shall open at any time for a vessel of 5000 gross tons and over, a vessel towing a vessel of 5000 gross tons and over, and a vessel proceeding to pick up for towing a vessel of 5000 gross tons and over. 33 CFR 117.1041(a)(1)

South Park Bridge

  • Opens on signal for boats.
  • On weekdays, except federal holidays other than Columbus Day, from 6:30 to 8:30 a.m. and 3:30 to 5:30 p.m. it need not open.
  • From 11 p.m. to 7 a.m., every day, it opens if at least 12 hours' notice is given by telephone or VHF radio to the South Park tender. If emergency responders need an opening in those hours, it opens within 45 minutes of the call to the Fremont Bridge operator.

The draws of each bridge across the Duwamish Waterway shall open on signal, except as follows: (2) The draw of the South Park Bridge, mile 3.8, need not be opened for the passage of vessels from 6:30 a.m. to 8:30 a.m. and from 3:30 p.m. to 5:30 p.m., Monday through Friday except, Federal holidays, other than Columbus Day. (3) Between the hours of 11 p.m. and 7 a.m., Monday through Sunday, the South Park Bridge shall open if at least a 12 hour notice is given by telephone or VHF radio to the drawtender at the South Park Bridge. If emergency responders require a bridge opening between 11 p.m. and 7 a.m., the South Park Bridge shall open within 45 minutes from initial notification to the Fremont Bridge operator. 33 CFR 117.1041(a)(2)-(3)

Southwest Spokane Street Bridge

  • Opens on the signal of one prolonged blast followed quickly by three short blasts.
  • The federal rule sets no closed hours for this bridge.
  • Seattle publishes no live up or down status for this bridge.

The following bridges shall open on the specified signals: (1) Burlington Northern Santa Fe railroad bridge, mile 0.4, and Southwest Spokane Street bridge, mile 0.3, one prolonged blast followed quickly by three short blasts. 33 CFR 117.1041(b)(1)

What the rules have in common

Every Seattle bridge can hold a lift up to 10 minutes to clear backed-up cars, unless the boat is a tug with a tow. Every weekday rush-hour exception has a size limit, 1,000 tons on the Ship Canal and 5,000 tons on the Duwamish, and a ship over that limit gets its opening no matter the hour. Federal holidays count as weekends, except Columbus Day, which the rule treats as a working day.

Questions

Do Seattle drawbridges open on signal?

Yes. Every one opens on a boat's signal, with weekday rush-hour exceptions for smaller vessels and, on some bridges, notice requirements at night.

What is the 10-minute rule?

A tender may delay an opening up to 10 minutes after a request to clear accumulated road traffic. The delay is not allowed for a vessel towing another.

Which rule has the most exceptions?

Montlake. Its weekday closed hours change with the season and it opens only on the hour and half hour through the early afternoon.
