Game-day facts

American Family Field sits on WIS 175 (Stadium Freeway) just south of I-94, west of downtown Milwaukee.

The I-94 East-West project runs through this stretch. WisDOT keeps an event page for Brewers 2026 with routes to the stadium: 94 East-West: Brewers 2026.

For crashes and closures elsewhere in the state, see Wisconsin road conditions. For every Milwaukee camera, see Milwaukee traffic cameras.
