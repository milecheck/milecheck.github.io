Winter driving

Why does Snoqualmie Pass close so often in winter?

Snoqualmie Pass closes for avalanche control when unstable snow threatens I-90. Crashes and winter road conditions can also close the pass. WSDOT closes the affected section of I-90 and checks that no vehicles remain. Crews then trigger avalanches before reopening the road. WSDOT says typical delays are 30 minutes to two hours.

Heavy snow, spinouts and crashes can also lead to closures. Vehicles without required traction equipment can cause traffic problems. (WSDOT) Check the live status on our Snoqualmie Pass page or Washington road conditions before you leave.

What happens during an avalanche closure?

  1. Crews close the road. A sweeper vehicle drives the zone to make sure no one is left in it.
  2. Explosives go on the slopes. Cable trams or artillery deliver them to the top of the avalanche paths. WSDOT now uses a 105mm howitzer when artillery is needed.
  3. Plows clear the road. Any snow that reached I-90 comes off before it reopens.

WSDOT says the typical delay is 30 minutes to two hours. In severe conditions it can be several hours. (WSDOT)

Why does it happen so often?

WSDOT reports average winter snowfall of more than 450 inches for the Snoqualmie and Stevens pass areas. Avalanche control is an ongoing winter operation. The most persistent avalanche zone is on the west side of the pass. (WSDOT) The summit sits at 3,022 feet, about 52 miles east of Seattle.

Closures can cause long backups on I-90. WSDOT counts about 28,000 vehicles a day over the pass, about 5,600 of them freight. (WSDOT) A long closure can cause backups in both directions.

What time of day does WSDOT close it?

There is no fixed hour. Crews try to work at night or when traffic is light, but the weather decides. (WSDOT) Planned closures go out through the WSDOT mobile app. WSDOT ended its separate I-90 text alerts on August 31, 2026. (WSDOT)

Can a chain sign close the pass?

Indirectly, yes. A vehicle that loses traction can block a lane and delay traffic. Ignoring a posted traction tire or chain requirement carries a $500 penalty. (RCW 47.36.250) Vehicles and combinations over 10,000 lb GVWR must carry chains on I-90 from milepost 32 to milepost 101 between November 1 and April 1. (WAC 204-24-050) Our chains required guide covers what each sign means.

How do I know if Snoqualmie Pass is closed right now?

  1. Read the pass report for your direction. WSDOT posts eastbound and westbound separately at wsdot.com. MileCheck reads the same WSDOT feed on our Snoqualmie Pass page.
  2. Look at the summit camera. A camera shows snow on the road. It does not tell you if the road is open, so read the report too.
  3. Fuel up before the climb. North Bend going east, Easton or Cle Elum going west. A closure can hold you for hours.

MileCheck's September 2026 report lists 53 I-90 events in Washington. Winter counts go in our monthly road report.

What is the detour if I-90 is closed?

US 2 crosses Stevens Pass to the north. US 12 crosses White Pass to the south. Check current conditions on either route before traveling. Both have their own snow, chain rules and closures. Neither is a short detour. (WSDOT) You may need to wait for I-90 to reopen.

Questions, answered

Why does Snoqualmie Pass close so often in winter?

Avalanche control is one reason WSDOT closes I-90 over Snoqualmie Pass. WSDOT closes the affected section and checks that no vehicles remain. Crews then trigger avalanches before reopening the road. WSDOT says the typical delay is 30 minutes to two hours. Heavy snow, crashes and spinouts can also close the pass.

How long does a Snoqualmie Pass avalanche closure last?

WSDOT says 30 minutes to two hours is typical. In severe conditions a closure can last several hours, because crews have to clear any snow that reached the road before it reopens.

What time does WSDOT do avalanche control on I-90?

There is no fixed time. Crews try to work at night or when traffic is light, but the weather decides when control is needed.

How do I find out if Snoqualmie Pass is closed?

Read the WSDOT Snoqualmie Pass report for your direction, or open the WSDOT mobile app and turn on Snoqualmie Pass I-90 notifications. WSDOT ended its I-90 text alerts on August 31, 2026.

What is the fine for ignoring a chain sign on Snoqualmie Pass?

$500 under RCW 47.36.250 for failing to obey a posted traction tire or chain requirement.

What is the detour if Snoqualmie Pass is closed?

US 2 over Stevens Pass and US 12 over White Pass are possible alternatives. Both can have winter restrictions and closures. Check WSDOT before changing your route.
